# Refined Recovery Experience — Atelier

**The kept idea:** *what you wove is woven.* Nothing done is ever undone by anything missed. Recovery is the emotional high point of the product, and every recovery screen must be calm, literal, and immediately operable — one decision, one obvious action, no guilt mechanics anywhere.

## The six situations

### 1 · One missed day
The cloth shows one open gap; the day after is woven normally. Copy: acknowledgement without ceremony ("Yesterday stayed open. Today is here."). Primary: **Pick up today** (strength) / **Write today** (writing). Secondary: "Make up yesterday instead — it will be mended in gold." A single missed day never generates a decision heavier than that sentence.

### 2 · Several missed days (same week)
Plain statement of fact with the cloth visible: the woven days named as kept, the open days named as open, no percentage, no "you're behind". Primary: **Continue from today**. Secondary: "Mend one missed day" (one, deliberately — mending is a gift, not a debt schedule). The week's theme line reminds what this week is teaching, because re-entry needs purpose, not arithmetic.

### 3 · Three-week absence
The signature screen. The cloth is shown **held** — rows 1–3 woven, rows 4–6 open, the remainder bare warp — under the headline built from the fixture's welcome-back voice. Three facts in plain text: what remains kept (never lost), where the programme now points (today, re-anchored), what happens to the open rows (they stay open; they may be mended; they never block). Then the capacity choice (below). No "restart", no "streak", no "you've missed 21 days" arithmetic.

### 4 · Lower-capacity return — the three levels
Borrowed as a principle from Meridian and re-expressed in Atelier's voice: three literal options, each restating **the next action it produces** so choosing is operating:

- **The full session** → "Begin — 14 minutes"
- **A shorter session** → "Begin — 8 minutes · counts in full"
- **The gentlest form** → "Begin — 5 minutes, seated · counts in full"

"Counts in full" appears on every reduced option — capacity is a setting, not a concession. The choice changes the *next screen's primary button label and session plan*, visibly, immediately. No option is pre-selected; no option is styled as the "right" one.

### 5 · Schedule adjustment
Literal control: **Change my weekly days** (in Programme & Support, and offered inline from the several-missed and long-absence states). The re-plan states its effect in one sentence before confirming ("Twelve weeks at three days a week — the same programme, ending later"). The weave language ("re-warps the loom") survives only as an italic aside, never as the control.

### 6 · Programme pause
**Pause the programme** — a real state, not abandonment. The pause screen says what pausing does (nothing is lost, the cloth holds, no charges arrive while paused — placeholder terms), shows the held cloth, and offers exactly one action: **Continue the programme** (plus the quiet "Change my weekly days" for people whose pause was really a schedule problem). Un-pausing re-anchors to today, never to the missed past.

## Rules that hold across all six

- The cloth is always visible in recovery — evidence, not accusation. Mended days render in gold stitch marks and are *named* mended, with the mend styled as care, never as correction.
- Every state pairs its visual with plain text; nothing relies on colour or textile knowledge.
- One decision per screen. Where a choice exists (capacity, mend-or-continue), choosing navigates immediately; there is no confirm step after a choice that already states its effect.
- No red anywhere in recovery. Open days are quiet paper-and-outline, not alarm.
- Copy bans: "streak", "broken", "lost", "behind", "restart", "fail". Verified against the fixtures.
- Reduced motion: mends and holds render in their completed state; nothing about recovery depends on an animation having played.
