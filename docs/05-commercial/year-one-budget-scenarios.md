# Year-One Budget Scenarios

**Status:** **Planning reference only — not a spending plan.** Superseded as spending authority by [`staged-capital-plan.md`](staged-capital-plan.md) under Confirmed founder decision D-010 (26 Jul 2026): capital is released gate by gate on evidence triggers (Gate A £0–100 → Gate E provisional £3,000–6,000 requiring a new founder decision). The scenarios below remain useful as *total-cost-of-quality* context — what a fully professional year costs if every specialist line eventually unlocks — and for calibrating individual line estimates. None of these totals is approved. Original estimating notes: UK freelance/contract mid-market rates, rounded; founder's own build skills priced in (no agency development line).

## What the money is actually for

With a founder who codes, year-one cash buys exactly six things: (1) credibility you cannot self-certify (expert programme review, accessibility audit, security check), (2) craft where specialist depth beats general skill (brand/visual design), (3) evidence (research incentives, landing tests), (4) hardware truth (device lab), (5) plumbing (services, accounts, tools), (6) contingency — because content and review always overrun (R-03).

## What each scenario honestly buys — and risks

- **Lean (~£8k):** everything ships, nothing is fake — but design is sprint-scoped (the territory must be executable by rules, not ongoing art direction), security is self-assessed, media is illustration-first, and the trademark screen is preliminary. Right if cash is genuinely tight; the risk concentrates in R-04 (premium perception) and slower Stage 3.
- **Standard (~£21.5k):** the recommendation *(Professional recommendation)* — proper expert review, a real design engagement, an external accessibility pass and a security review. This is the cheapest version that defends every brand promise in the docs.
- **Comfortable (~£41k):** adds the second programme's review (platform proof at launch), video-grade demonstrations, deeper security and legal cover. Right only if funding is comfortable — the *product* risk it retires is modest; most of the delta is polish and insurance.

## Cash-flow shape (any scenario)

Spend is gated, not front-loaded: Stage 1 needs only incentives + tools (≈£600–1,200). The big lines (design, expert review) unlock **after** Gate 1→2 evidence says proceed — so the real year-one exposure if Stage 1 says *stop* is under £1,500 in every scenario. Quality-gates-over-dates (your stated priority) also means lines can shift across months freely.

## Not in these numbers

Your time (15–20 h/week is the largest real investment); any paid user acquisition at scale (post-validation decision); Apple/Google revenue share (comes out of revenue, not budget); incorporation/accounting (jurisdiction-dependent, typically £300–1,500/yr — add if not already handled).
