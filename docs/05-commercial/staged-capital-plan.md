# Staged Capital Plan

**Status:** Active — implements **Confirmed founder decision D-010** (gated capital), 26 July 2026. This document is the **spending authority**; [`year-one-budget-scenarios.md`](year-one-budget-scenarios.md) remains a planning reference only. Principle: **professional quality is deferred until evidence justifies it, never removed.** Founder skills and existing tools are used first; specialist money buys focused reviews, not broad engagements.

## The gates

| Gate | Stage | Cumulative spend authority | New approval needed to pass |
|------|-------|----------------------------|------------------------------|
| **A** | Foundation & evidence synthesis *(current)* | **£0–£100** | — (in force) |
| **B** | Passive concept & landing validation | **≤£300 cumulative** without new approval | Founder go to publish/spend |
| **C** | Interactive prototype | £500–£1,000 cumulative target | Founder gate review |
| **D** | Private beta | £1,000–£1,500 cumulative target | Founder gate review |
| **E** | Public launch | Provisional £3,000–£6,000 route | **New founder decision required** before any commitment |

Rules: cumulative caps are hard — nothing rolls forward silently; any single expense above £50 inside a gate is logged in the weekly summary; any expense that would breach the gate's cumulative cap requires founder approval *before* commitment, not after; all spend records live with the validation dashboard.

## What this plan refuses to do *(quality-protection clauses, per founder instruction)*

No device lab before beta · no expensive media before the guidance format is validated · no full security review before a real architecture exists · no flagship reviewer before the flagship is selected · no full visual-design engagement before an art direction and core experience pass prototype review · and equally: **no shipping past Gate E without the accessibility review, security review and expert content review** — deferral has an end date, and it is the public launch gate.

## Reporting

Every gate review presents: cumulative spend vs authority, per-line actuals, upcoming unlocks with their evidence triggers, and any request to move a cap. The weekly evidence summary carries the running spend line (validation dashboard).
