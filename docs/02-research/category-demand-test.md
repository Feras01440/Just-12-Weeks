# Category Demand Test

**Status:** Ready; runs inside the landing experiment's post-signup step (not published). Purpose: real-behaviour evidence for **Q1** (flagship launch category) across the four programme archetypes — informing, never deciding, the founder's choice.

## 1. The vote

Post-signup screen: *"Which would you most want a guided twelve-week programme for? Pick one."* Four propositions, one per archetype, phrased as concrete outcomes (not categories), order randomised per viewer:

| Archetype | Proposition (v1 wording) |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Physical/wellbeing | "Get genuinely stronger and fitter from a standing start" |
| Skill-learning | "Learn to code enough to build something real" |
| Practical/creative | "Write the first draft of the book you keep not writing" |
| Routine/personal development | "Rebuild your sleep, focus and daily routine" |

Plus: "Something else →" (single choice from a longer fixed list: language speaking · an instrument · running your first 5K–10K · portfolio/career project · decluttering & finishing projects — capturing spread without free text).

**Wording rules:** each proposition is claims-policy safe, concrete, and archetype-faithful; wordings are frozen per test round (changing them starts a new round — results never mix across wordings; version stamped in the event).

## 2. Second signal: "would you start when?"

One optional follow-up chip row: "If this existed, when would you realistically start? — this month / next few months / someday / just curious." Separates warm demand from idle curiosity in the concentration reading (a category winning on "just curious" votes is a weaker Q1 signal, and the dashboard reports the split).

## 3. Reading rules

- Verdict rules live in **[passive-validation-plan §4](passive-validation-plan.md)** (canonical, pre-registered): read at the fixed date only, on **n ≥ 100 qualified votes** (organic, confirmed, current wording version), requiring winner share **≥35% and a ≥12-point margin over the runner-up**, quoted with its confidence interval. **The former "25–35% = leading" band is void** — 25% is a four-option ballot's equal-share null, so shares near it are reported as "insufficient evidence", never as a lean. Whichever candidate wins gets its pre-specified cheap second test before Q1 goes to the founder (symmetric follow-up rule). A flat spread routes to Extend/Escalate per §4.5; one v2-wording revision starts a *new round* (results never pool across wordings).
- Triangulation: read alongside (a) the public-evidence corpus's unmet-need findings (Q10) and (b) the flagship-opportunity analysis's non-demand factors (safety, cost, guidance-fit) — **demand is one input among fifteen**, and the analysis document owns the weighing.
- Age-band lens: where the optional survey provides bands, concentration is also read per band **once a band holds ≥10 qualified votes** (below that: "insufficient n", no cross-band claims) — a category winning only under-30 is a different Q1 story than one winning across bands (D-001 relevance).
- Fraud hygiene: one vote per confirmed email; duplicate/disposable flags per passive-plan §3.5.

## 4. What this test cannot establish *(stated for reuse honesty)*

Willingness to pay for the winning category (pricing test approximates interest only) · completion appetite · whether the *guidance depth* the category needs is buildable within capital gates (the opportunity analysis owns that) · anything about users who never reach a landing page (channel bias — logged in research-limitations.md).
