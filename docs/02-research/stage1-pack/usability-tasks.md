# Age-Inclusive Usability Test Tasks (Pre-Registered for Stage 5)

**Status:** Pre-registered now (so Stage-5 success can't be redefined after the prototype exists); executed at Stage 5 on the interactive prototype. Regime per the aligned G6 standard: **10–12 participants, every age band n≥2, ≥2 low-digital-confidence, ≥80% task success overall with no age cohort's majority failing.** Conditions across the set: ≥2 sessions at 200% text · ≥1 reduced-motion · ≥1 screen-reader-primary participant · daily-loop tasks attempted one-handed · device familiar to the participant where possible (their platform, not our demo phone).

## Protocol notes

Think-aloud invited but never forced ("talk me through it if that's comfortable"); no rescuing before the fail criterion; every task scored pass / pass-with-struggle / fail + verbatim confusion notes; wording below is read exactly as written (no helpful paraphrase — the wording *is* the test); order fixed T1→T10 except T8 (paywall) which always runs before fatigue (slot ≤6th).

## The tasks

| # | Task (read aloud) | Pass = | Fail signal watched |
|---|-------------------|--------|---------------------|
| T1 | "You've just downloaded this app. Take a look and tell me what it is and who it's for." | States guide/programme idea in own words, without "tracker/dashboard" framing | CS-TRACKER perception; value screens skipped-and-lost |
| T2 | "Find a programme that might suit you and tell me whether it's right for you — and how you'd know." | Reaches programme detail; cites suitability info and reviewer/credentials unprompted or on light probe | Governance metadata invisible; suitability read as marketing |
| T3 | "Start the programme." (Suitability screen included) | Completes screening honestly; understands any advise-against respectfully | Screening read as interrogation; G4 breach observations |
| T4 | "It's your first day. Show me what you're supposed to do today — and how you'd know how to do it properly." | Opens guided action; finds steps + demonstration + why | G1 failure; player navigation confusion |
| T5 | "Today the standard version feels like too much. What would you do?" | Finds the easier variant without prompting; describes it without shame framing | Variant lane invisible; "easier" read as demotion |
| T6 | "Mark today as done." | Completes in ≤2 interactions from the player | G2 breach; confirmation ambiguity |
| T7 | "Imagine you've been away for four days. Open the app and tell me what's happening and what you'd do." | Retells the recovery conversation accurately (RC-BELIEVED-style); picks a door | Guilt perception; door overload; disbelief |
| T8 | "You've decided you want full access. Go as far as you can toward paying, then stop at the last step — and tell me exactly what it costs, how often, and how you'd cancel." | **G9: states price, period, renewal and cancellation unaided — 5/5 sampled, non-negotiable** | Any misstatement = paywall redesign, regardless of task completion |
| T9 | "Find where you'd pause the whole programme for a holiday — and what pausing does." | Reaches pause ≤3 taps from Today/Journey; states clock-stops semantics | Pause conflated with quitting; semantics unclear |
| T10 | "Show me how you'd get all your information out of the app, and how you'd delete your account — and tell me what deletion does and doesn't do." | Finds export and deletion; **states that store subscription must be cancelled separately** | The category's classic harm reproduced = blocker |

## Per-band analysis (D-001 teeth)

Results tabled per band and per condition (200%, reduced-motion, screen-reader, one-handed) — not just overall. Any task where a band's majority fails or where all low-confidence participants struggle triggers a design revision + retest of that task before Gate 5→6, even if the overall ≥80% holds. Verbatim confusion quotes feed the content-strategy plain-language revision loop.

## Outputs

Task-level scores into the Stage-5 findings report; G6/G9 verdicts into milestone-gates Gate 5→6; surprises into the assumptions register; and every failed wording becomes a tracked copy fix (the test's words were the product's words).
