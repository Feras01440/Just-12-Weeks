# Screener and Recruitment

**Status:** Execution-ready. Eligibility per D-006 (Age-inclusive Restarters); quotas per D-001. Nothing here has been posted anywhere — posting is the founder's explicit go.

## 1. Eligibility screener (form, ~2 minutes, 8 questions)

Form intro (neutral — no product pitch): *"I'm researching how adults pursue personal goals that take months of effort — things like getting fitter, learning a skill, or building a routine. It's a 40-minute conversation, £[X] thank-you voucher, no sales involved, and everything you share stays confidential."*

| # | Question | Type | Qualifies if |
|---|----------|------|--------------|
| S1 | In the last 18 months, have you started working toward a personal goal that needed weeks or months of regular effort? (e.g. fitness, learning something, a project, a routine) | Yes/No | Yes |
| S2 | What was the goal, in a few words? | Short text | Any genuine slow-results goal (screen out one-off tasks) |
| S3 | What happened? | Choice: still going strong / kept going but it was hard / stopped before I wanted to / stopped and restarted at least once | **"stopped before I wanted to" or "stopped and restarted" = Restarter core**; "kept going but hard" = eligible secondary; "still going strong" = reserve list |
| S4 | Did you spend any money on it? (apps, memberships, courses, equipment, coaching) | Multi-choice + rough amount band | Any answer (spend recorded, not required) |
| S5 | Your age group | 18–29 / 30–49 / 50–64 / 65+ | Quota routing |
| S6 | How comfortable are you learning a new app on your own? | Very / fairly / not very / not at all | Track; recruit ≥3 "not very/not at all" overall |
| S7 | Do you work in fitness coaching, dietetics, dermatology, dentistry, or app design/development? | Yes/No | **No** (professional-lens screen-out) |
| S8 | Contact + preferred time + consent to be contacted about this research only | Contact fields | Required |

Automatic screen-outs: under 18; S7 = yes; close friends/family of the founder (pilot-only); anyone who cannot consent freely. Reserve list ("still going strong"): up to 2 interviews for contrast, outside quota.

## 3. Neutral recruitment messages (copy-ready; no product claims, no pressure)

**Community post (Reddit/Facebook groups/Discord — with moderator permission, always):**
> "Research interview (£[X] voucher): did you start a fitness plan, course, or big personal goal in the last year or so — and find it hard to keep going? I'm doing independent product research into how adults pursue goals that take months, and I'd love 40 minutes on a video call to hear what actually happened (no judgement — the messy version is the useful version). Not selling anything; your details stay private and are deleted after the research. 2-minute eligibility form: [link]"

**Second-degree personal ask (text/DM to your contacts):**
> "Quick favour — I'm researching how people pursue goals that take months (fitness, learning, projects). Do you know *one person* (not you!) who started something like that this year and found it hard to stick with? I'd pay them a £[X] voucher for a 40-minute chat. Happy to send the 2-minute form."

**Offline notice (gym/library/community-centre/U3A-style boards — with permission):**
> "PAID RESEARCH CHAT (£[X] voucher) — Have you ever started getting fit, learning a skill or building a new routine… and lost momentum? Researcher seeks adults of all ages (18+) for a friendly 40-minute conversation about how it really went. Phone, video or in a public café — your choice. No sales, fully confidential. Text/email: [contact] or scan: [QR to form]"

**65+-friendly variant (for community channels):** same as offline notice, plus: "You don't need to be 'good with technology' — that's part of what I'm trying to understand."

Message rules: never name the app concept; never imply their past attempts were failures ("lost momentum", "how it really went" — not "why you failed"); state the voucher, the length, the confidentiality and the no-sales promise every time; every channel post logged (where, when, wording) in the recruitment log.

## 4. Channels (in cost order)

1. Second-degree network sweep (free; strongest show-up rates).
2. Community posts with permission: r/getdisciplined, r/decidingtobebetter, r/fitness30plus, r/learnprogramming, local-town Facebook groups, relevant Discords.
3. Offline: gym/library/community boards; U3A-style and hobby groups for the 65+ band.
4. Snowball: the close of every interview asks for one referral.
5. Paid panels (Respondent-class, ~£40–60/participant) **only** if a quota band stalls two weeks — founder approves before spend (incentives-and-budget).

## 5. Scheduling & logistics

Offer video (default), phone, or public-place in-person (65+/low-confidence friendliness); 45-minute slots, max 2/day (note quality dies on the third); confirmation message includes the consent summary (consent-and-privacy §1) so nobody is surprised on the call; no-show policy: one polite reschedule, then release the slot.
