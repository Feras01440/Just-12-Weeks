# Stage 1 Decision Criteria — Proceed / Pivot / Stop

**Status:** Pre-registered 26 July 2026, **before any participant is contacted**. Scope updated under D-009: these interview-based criteria apply **when an interview escalation runs**; the *default* Gate 1→2 verdict is written against the passive system's pre-registered thresholds ([passive-validation-plan §4](../passive-validation-plan.md)) with this page's stop conditions (S1–S5) still governing where referenced. Escalated rounds report against the smallest sufficient subset of P1–P4. Sample honesty: with 9–12 interviews these are strong directional signals, not statistics — which is why the criteria demand *unprompted, specific, past-behaviour* evidence rather than polite agreement.

## PROCEED (all five required)

| # | Criterion | Threshold | Source |
|---|-----------|-----------|--------|
| P1 | The problem is real and felt | ≥7 of the Restarter-core interviews (min 7 core) describe abandonment-before-results **unprompted, with a specific past story** (PR-GAP/PR-COLLAPSE/PR-RESTART coded from section B, not E) | Interviews |
| P2 | The category monetises from lived behaviour | ≥5 participants show real past spend on structure for their goal (MO-STRUCTURE or MO-SPENDER ≥£50) | Interviews C9–10 |
| P3 | Guidance is the wanted shape | HS-DIRECT + HS-SHOW outnumber HS-SCORE among 11a answers | Interviews |
| P4 | The recovery bet lands | In E2: RC-BELIEVED clearly outnumbers RC-DISBELIEF, **and** ≥3 participants whose honest D13 baseline was quit/start-something-new (RC-BASELINE-QUIT) react to the recovery message with belief or "which door" engagement | Interviews |
| P5 | Cold-audience floor | ≥3% blended landing signup on ≥300 visitors | Landing test |

Plus a soft signal (not gating): the 12-week frame reads FR-MOTIVATING more than FR-PRESSURE across bands (checked per band — a frame that motivates 25-year-olds and pressures 65-year-olds is a *design* finding, not a pass).

## PIVOT triggers (evidence says: something real, but not this shape — founder chooses the pivot, work re-plans)

| # | Signal | The pivot it points to |
|---|--------|------------------------|
| V1 | Problem confirmed but help-shape is HS-COMPANY dominant (they want humans/company, not direction) | Accountability/cohort-led product model — different engagement architecture, revisit N-20 posture before Stage 2 |
| V2 | FR-PRESSURE dominates the 12-week frame overall | Reframe the container (rolling starts, 4-week blocks, softer horizon) — core promise language reworked before Stage 2 |
| V3 | Demand concentrates hard in one *unexpected* category (e.g. skills ≥35% while physical interest is thin) | Q1 leans to the evidenced category; governance load re-planned (a genuinely good outcome — cheaper safety posture) |
| V4 | P1 passes but P2 fails (problem felt, wallet absent in this pattern) | Revisit segment emphasis or price architecture (free-led + later monetisation) before committing Stage 2 scope |
| V5 | Recovery believed but "another tracker" perception persists in E1 (CS-TRACKER coded ≥4 times) | Positioning pivot: lead H3/recovery in all messaging; brand work re-sequenced before wireframes |

## STOP (any one, verified against the evidence board, stops Stage 2 spend)

| # | Signal |
|---|--------|
| S1 | P1 fails: the abandonment problem doesn't surface unprompted with specific stories — the thesis's foundation is absent |
| S2 | P2 fails **and** V4's re-analysis finds no credible monetisation path (pattern demonstrably won't pay in any structure) |
| S3 | RC-DISBELIEF dominates E2 across bands — the differentiator reads as manipulation, and the honest version of this product has no wedge |
| S4 | Landing signup <1.5% blended after message iteration across all three arms — cold demand materially absent |
| S5 | Recruitment integrity fails: quotas cannot be met after documented multi-channel effort, leaving D-001's audience claim untestable (stop-and-rethink recruitment, not necessarily the product — but no proceeding on an unrepresentative sample) |

## Verdict mechanics

The verdict is written as one page: each criterion → met/not-met → the three strongest evidence items each way (P-codes, quotes) → per-band notes → the recommendation. Mixed results go to the founder as *pivot options with evidence*, never silently reinterpreted as passes. Whatever the outcome, `validation-evidence.md` is filled and the decision-log entry cites this page's version.
