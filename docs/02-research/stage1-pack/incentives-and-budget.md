# Stage 1 Incentives and Cash Plan

**Status:** Scenarios for founder selection — no spend authorised by this document. Feeds the year-one picture in [`05-commercial/year-one-budget-scenarios.md`](../../05-commercial/year-one-budget-scenarios.md); this page is Stage 1 only.

## Incentive principles

Pay people for their time, every time, including withdrawals and pilots-who-thought-they-were-participants — respect is cheaper than bias. Vouchers (supermarket/multi-store), not cash transfers (simpler, no banking data per consent-and-privacy). Same rate for every participant regardless of band or channel (no haggling, no tiering by "value"). The 65+/offline channels may prefer physical vouchers — allow it, log it.

## Scenarios

| Line | Minimal | Recommended | Buffered |
|------|--------:|------------:|---------:|
| Interview voucher rate × 9–12 | £15 × 9 = £135 | £20 × 12 = £240 | £25 × 12 = £300 |
| Pilot session thank-you (1, excluded from data) | £0 (friendly favour) | £15 | £20 |

| Stalled-band panel recruits (Respondent-class, only on founder go) | £0 | £0 (hold) | £150 (≈3 seats) |
| Landing paid traffic top-up (**separate founder go required**) | £0 | £0 (hold; organic-first) | £150 |
| Tools (form + email double-opt-in + page hosting, ~2 months) | £0–20 (free tiers) | £40 | £60 |
| Contingency | £20 | £40 | £60 |
| **Stage 1 total** | **≈ £175** | **≈ £365** | **≈ £770** |

**Recommendation *(Professional recommendation)*:** the £365 line — £20 respects 40–45 minutes of an adult's time at any age, referral thank-yous keep the cheapest channel warm, and the holds keep paid options one message away without committing them.

## Controls

Voucher issue logged same-day (who/when/amount — the D-record from consent-and-privacy §2); weekly dashboard tracks spend vs the chosen cap; any line moving >20% or any "hold" activating = a founder decision, not a drift. Nothing in this stage requires an account, subscription or service beyond the tools line — if a tool wants more, it's the wrong tool.
