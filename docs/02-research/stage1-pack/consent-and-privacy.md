# Consent and Participant Privacy

**Status:** Execution-ready. Plain-language consent (content-strategy voice) + the data-handling procedure behind it. The research must live up to the product's privacy standards before the product exists.

## 1. Informed-participation script (read aloud at the start of every session; also sent with the booking confirmation)

> "Before we start, the honest version of what this is. I'm doing early research for a possible mobile app that helps people work toward personal goals. I'm not selling anything today, and there are no right or wrong answers — the messy, real version of your experience is exactly what helps.
>
> A few promises: everything you tell me is confidential. Your name never appears in any findings — if I quote you, it'll be anonymous, like 'a participant in their 50s'. You can skip any question, take a break, or stop completely at any point, no explanation needed, and you'll still receive the thank-you voucher.
>
> **Recording:** with your permission, I'd like to record audio so I can listen properly instead of scribbling. Only I will hear the recording; it's stored securely, used only to write up notes, and deleted within 90 days — sooner if you ask. You can say no and we'll just talk while I take notes. Is recording okay? *(Record the verbal yes/no on tape if yes: "It's [date], you've agreed to the recording — thank you.")*
>
> And if you change your mind after today — about the recording or about taking part at all — message me and I'll delete your data completely. Any questions before we begin?"

Written consent: the booking confirmation carries the same points as short bullets + the contact for withdrawal; a reply of "confirmed" is sufficient record for this research class (no signature ceremony that intimidates — proportionality).

## 2. Participant privacy & data-retention procedure

| Data | Where it lives | Retention | Notes |
|------|----------------|-----------|-------|
| Screener responses (incl. contact) | One access-controlled form/sheet, founder-only | Deleted ≤30 days after Stage 1 verdict (kept during stage for scheduling/quotas) | Never reused for marketing; the form says "contacted about this research only" and that is the law |
| Recordings (audio) | Encrypted local folder + backed-up copy, founder-only; **never** uploaded to third-party transcription without a checked DPA (default: no third-party transcription — listen and note manually) | Deleted ≤90 days after session, or immediately on request | Filename = P-code, never a name |
| Interview notes | One file per participant (P01…P12), P-code only; the name↔P-code key lives in a separate access-controlled file | Notes (anonymised) kept as `validation-evidence.md` sources; **key file deleted ≤30 days after verdict** — after which nothing is re-identifiable | Verbatim quotes marked at capture; quotes carrying identifying detail paraphrased or dropped |
| Landing-page emails | The signup tool, double-opt-in wording ("early-access updates only") | Kept while the project is live; every mail has working unsubscribe; list deleted if the project stops | Never joined to interview data |
| Voucher records | Minimal (who was paid, when, amount) | 24 months (simple accounting) | Separate from research data |
| Withdrawal requests | Actioned ≤7 days: recording, notes, screener row and key entry erased; dashboard counts adjusted with a "withdrawn" marker, content removed | — | Withdrawal never claws back the voucher |

Rules of conduct: no interviews with anyone who cannot freely consent; distress protocol — if a session touches something raw (goal histories can), pause, offer to stop or move on, never probe the raw spot for "good data"; safeguarding common sense — public-place or remote sessions only; no under-18s (screener-enforced). Legal basis: consent, recorded plainly; this procedure is the fulfilment mechanism, and it inherits the product privacy model's spirit (minimisation, purpose limitation, honest deletion) at research scale.
