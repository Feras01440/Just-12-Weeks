# Customer Language Bank

**Status:** Stage 1A instrument. Real users' words (as indexed; ≤25-word fair-quotation excerpts, corpus row refs attached) organised for product and copy work. **Usage rules:** these phrases inform *our understanding and testing stimuli*; they are never quoted in marketing as testimonials, never attributed to our product, and paraphrase-marked rows are not quotation candidates at all. The bank exists because the product's voice should answer the customer's voice — precisely.

## The pain, in their words

**On not knowing what to do:** "instead of scratching my head" (EV-2042) · "I don't know how [to] ensure that a workout I would build is healthiest" (EV-2044) · "so many workout apps, and they're all a bit confusing" (EV-2045) · [para] beginners "fail because they don't know what to do" (EV-2046).
**On logging burden:** "a slog to use" (EV-2001) · [para] "6–10 taps for what took 2–3" (EV-2002) · [para] "a 90-second routine to a 5-minute chore" (EV-2004) · "Sometimes I feel like not logging things because I know it's really unhealthy" (EV-2007).
**On streaks and lapses:** "like losing a limb" (EV-1038) · "it hurts" (EV-1039) · "Lost another streak...incredibly frustrated!" **On billing betrayal:** "deceptive dark pattern design and outright fraud" (EV-3010) · "MasterClass is an ANNUAL auto-renew subscription and they DO NOT refund anyone" (EV-3016) · "I had no idea of it being an annual subscription" (EV-3047) · "I don't want to spend my remaining years fussing with companies over this." ## The desire, in their words

**Guidance as relief:** "I feel safe and confident and don't have to think about the incremental steps" (EV-2018) · "Is very calming to know what to do next" (EV-2040) · "Adaptive Training removes any guess work and lets me just train" (EV-2020) · "It's all taken care of… No thinking. Just work." (EV-2039) · "I never have to think about what exercises I should do" (EV-2037).
**Accountability as the paid thing:** "the only method I've ever found that has consistently gotten me in the gym… months in a row" (EV-3032) · "the accountability keeps me consistent" (EV-3033) · [para] "a good program that keeps you accountable and motivated is worth it" (EV-3031).
**Forgiveness noticed:** "No guilt from the app if you go over" (EV-2014) · [para] "the bird never dies" — support, not guilt, on missed days (EV-1035).
**Finishing as identity:** "graduated and can now comfortably run 5k, or even 10k" (EV-3041) · "This is the longest I have ever stuck with any exercise routine in my life" (EV-3043) · [para] first post: "too heavy to run?" → full 5K within months (EV-1004).
**Premium, described:** "the UI is absolutely gorgeous" (EV-2050) · "gentle (as promised), affirming, and quietly powerful" (EV-2049) · "a delightful interface that never gets in the way" (EV-2058) · "Satisfying to fill out, and beautiful to look at" (EV-2057).

## Words the market taught users to distrust *(anti-lexicon input)*

"free trial" (heard as: card trap — EV-3001…3003) · "personalised" (heard as: quiz-to-paywall; generic output — EV-3026, EV-3027) · "coach" without a human (heard as: "a facade" — EV-2033) · "limited time" (regulator-named manipulation) · "senior-friendly" (heard as: you think I'm less capable — EV-4039) · "cancel anytime" unaccompanied by *how* (heard as: famous last words — EV-3005, EV-3013).

## How this bank is used

Copy testing stimuli (which phrasings our audience already carries) · interview/unmoderated probe wording (mirror their language, never our jargon) · content-strategy voice calibration (the product answers "scratching my head" with "know exactly what to do today" — the promise is literally the customer's sentence inverted) · claims-policy sensitivity (the distrusted-words list extends the banned lexicons contextually). Every entry keeps its row ref so provenance survives reuse.
