# Unmoderated Testing Plan (Stage 5 preparation)

**Status:** Designed now so Stage 5 can run without founder-led moderation by default (D-008/D-009); executes only when an interactive prototype exists (Gate C). Moderated sessions remain the escalation for what unmoderated methods cannot diagnose — especially age-inclusive failures (D-009 trigger four).

## 1. Method

Task-based unmoderated tests on the clickable prototype: participants complete the **pre-registered task set** (`stage1-pack/usability-tasks.md` T1–T10 — unchanged, single source) self-paced on their own device, with think-aloud screen+voice recording where the participant consents, or silent completion + structured post-task questions where recording is declined. Distribution via unmoderated-testing tooling on free/cheap tiers (register-compliant; Gate C cap) or direct prototype links with an instruction sheet for volunteers.

## 2. Recruitment without founder interviews

Waitlist volunteers first (the passive system's cohort — invited by an approved template, thank-you voucher ~£10 per staged-capital caps); shortfalls filled per band from volunteer communities; the **65+ and low-digital-confidence quotas remain mandatory** (D-001) — if unmoderated recruitment cannot fill them after documented effort, that is itself a D-009 escalation (panel-recruited or moderated sessions for those bands, founder decision, ~£30–60/participant line in the capital plan).

## 3. What unmoderated testing measures well — and what it cannot

**Well:** task success/failure against T1–T10 pass criteria · G2 tap-count reality · G9 paywall comprehension (typed answers to "what does it cost / how often / how cancel") · first-reaction comprehension (T1 verbatim answers) · where people stall (recording timestamps).
**Cannot (escalation territory):** *why* an older adult abandoned T4 (diagnosis needs conversation) · distress or emotional reads on the recovery flow · anything requiring probing past a scripted question. The plan's honesty rule: unmoderated failures in the 65+/accessibility cohort are **diagnosed via moderated escalation, never guessed at** from recordings.

## 4. Protocol specifics

- Participant instructions written at reading age ~9–11, tested on one pilot volunteer first; instructions never explain the product beyond the standard T1 framing (comprehension is the test).
- Conditions distributed across the cohort per the usability-tasks regime: ≥2 sessions at 200% text, ≥1 reduced-motion, ≥1 screen-reader-primary (screen-reader sessions are the most likely moderated-escalation candidates — attempted unmoderated only with an experienced AT-user volunteer).
- Scoring: two independent passes over each recording/response set (maker–checker: the implementer scores, the independent reviewer samples ≥30% for agreement; disagreements resolved conservatively).
- Consent and data handling per `stage1-pack/consent-and-privacy.md` §2 (recordings 90-day deletion, P-codes, withdrawal rights) — unchanged standards, unmoderated context.

## 5. Outputs and gates

Task-level results feed the Stage-5 findings report and Gate 5→6 exactly as the moderated plan would have (G6/G9 verdicts per the aligned regime); per-band tables mandatory; every failed wording becomes a tracked copy fix. The gate pack states plainly which results came from unmoderated methods and which required escalation — evidence provenance is part of the evidence.
