## Evidence-weighted ranking

1. **C-A Beginner strength foundations** — the deepest verified problem + the proven wallet + native guidance fit. Its costs (governance, reviewer, media) are real but *scheduled* by the capital plan (reviewer unlocks at Gate D, after evidence).
2. **C-B First-draft writing** — the timing opportunity: a verified structural vacuum, the cheapest credible build, the cleanest D-003 evidence model, the lowest safety surface, and the highest remarkability per pound. Held back from #1 only by unsized demand and thinner app-format WTP precedent.
3. **C-C Routine reset** — broadest and cheapest, but weakest measurable-progress story and fuzziest finish line; better as a *second* programme where its softness is buffered by an established completion culture.
4. **C-D Coding** — founder-skill fit is real, but the free-incumbent shadow, off-app work pattern (O-08's strongest case) and weak structure-WTP make it the hardest first commercial proof.

**(C-E Language speaking, briefly):** the user-voice gap is loud (drills don't produce speech — E4-01…04) and genuinely tempting, but honest daily *speaking* guidance demands pedagogy and media investment beyond early capital gates, against massive incumbents. Logged as a future candidate once the engine and capital position mature.

## Reversible recommendation *(Professional recommendation — NOT a decision; Q1 stays open)*

**Carry C-A (provisional lead) and C-B (designated challenger) into the category-demand test as the two live flagship candidates**, with C-C and C-D remaining on the ballot as archetype coverage. Decision rule proposed for the Q1 memo: ≥25% organic concentration for either lead candidate confirms it; a C-B win or near-tie triggers the writing-community landing variant before final choice (its cheap test); a flat spread reopens C-C/C-E per the passive plan's revise path. Under the gated-capital model, note honestly: **if Gate-C capital stays minimal, C-B is the cheapest credible first build**; if the vote and wallet evidence favour C-A, its reviewer/media costs are affordable at Gate D by design. Either outcome preserves D-004: the engine ships archetype-complete regardless.
