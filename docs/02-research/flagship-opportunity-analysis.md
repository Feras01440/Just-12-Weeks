## Ranking *(professional judgement over the cited rows — not a computed score, and not a decision; Q1 stays open)*

1. **C-A Beginner strength foundations** — first on a narrower margin than this document previously implied. The problem evidence is the deepest (P-1) and the guidance fit native; but the "proven wallet" is proven for **human-delivered** formats (EV-3032, EV-3033, EV-3044) while app-subscription WTP stays unproven; the credentialed review prices at or beyond the whole Gate-D envelope; and under D-001 its 65+ safety burden is the heaviest of the four.
2. **C-B First-draft writing** — the timing opportunity: a verified shutdown (EV-4009) with an evidence-supported gap behind it (EV-4010), the cheapest credible build, the cleanest D-003 evidence model, the lowest safety surface, and the highest remarkability per pound. Held back by unsized demand, thin app-format WTP precedent — and an off-app work pattern (O-08) it shares with coding, which earlier versions of this table charged to C-D alone.
3. **C-C Routine reset** — broadest and cheapest, but the weakest user-voice problem evidence in the corpus (supply-side new-entrant signals more than pain verbatims), the weakest measurable-progress story and the fuzziest finish line; better as a *second* programme where its softness is buffered by an established completion culture.
4. **C-D Coding** — founder-skill fit is real and paid-structure demand exists off-app (EV-3039), but the free-incumbent shadow, the shared off-app pattern and unproven app-format WTP make it the hardest first commercial proof.

**What changed in the audit-response revision (26 Jul 2026):** the ranking heading no longer claims to be "evidence-weighted" as if computed; C-A's wallet evidence is split human-vs-app; its reviewer cost is restated against the real Gate-D envelope; its 65+ burden is stated as a cost of D-001, not a free strength; O-08 is applied symmetrically to C-B and C-D; C-C's problem evidence is downgraded to match the corpus. The order survives these corrections, but the C-A–C-B gap is materially narrower than previously presented.

**(C-E Language speaking, briefly):** the user-voice gap is loud (drills don't produce speech — EV-4001…4004) and genuinely tempting, but honest daily *speaking* guidance demands pedagogy and media investment beyond early capital gates, against massive incumbents. Logged as a future candidate once the engine and capital position mature.

## Reversible recommendation *(Professional recommendation — NOT a decision; Q1 stays open)*

**Carry C-A (provisional lead) and C-B (designated challenger) into the category-demand test as the two live flagship candidates**, with C-C and C-D remaining on the ballot as archetype coverage.

**The decision rule formerly proposed here — "≥25% organic concentration for either lead candidate confirms it" — is void.** 25% is the equal-share null of a four-option ballot: clearing it is indistinguishable from a coin-flip spread and confirms nothing. The Q1 verdict is governed solely by the canonical pre-registered protocol in [passive-validation-plan §4](passive-validation-plan.md) (qualified-vote floor, winner-share and margin thresholds, fixed read date, Extend / Escalate / Stop). Under that protocol: a C-B win or qualifying near-tie still triggers the writing-community landing variant before final choice (its cheap test); a below-threshold spread routes to Extend or Escalate — never to a pick.

Under the gated-capital model, note honestly: **if Gate-C capital stays minimal, C-B is the cheapest credible first build.** If the vote and wallet evidence favour C-A, its reviewer/media costs are **not** simply "affordable at Gate D" as this document previously said — they price at or beyond the whole Gate-D envelope, so a C-A selection implies either a scoped single-cycle review inside Gate D's ceiling or a Gate-E release, which is a new founder decision under D-010. Either outcome preserves D-004: the engine ships archetype-complete regardless.
