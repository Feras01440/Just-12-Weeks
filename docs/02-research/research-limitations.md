# Research Limitations (Stage 1A)

**Status:** Standing honesty document, 26 July 2026. Every consumer of the Stage-1A evidence set reads this first. The corpus is genuinely useful *and* genuinely limited; both halves are stated.

## 1. Retrieval-layer limitations (apply to every corpus row)

- **Snippet-only retrieval:** the research environment's egress proxy blocked direct page fetches on effectively every content host (app stores, Trustpilot, Reddit, forums, blogs, even Wikipedia). Every row is search-snippet retrieval of indexed content: quotes are as-indexed, could not be checked against full pages, and surrounding context is unseen. Confidence columns already discount for this; nothing here should be externally quoted without primary re-verification.
- **Reddit is effectively absent** at first hand (not indexed by the available engine; direct fetch blocked) — the category's richest user-voice venue enters only via journalists/aggregators. This skews sourcing toward Trustpilot/BBB/blogs/forums that *are* indexed.
- **Dates are missing for most rows** (snippets omit them); "undated" rows should be read as persistent-through-2024–26 page content, not confirmed-current sentiment.
- **Language/market skew:** virtually all evidence is English (US/UK/AU); nothing here speaks for non-Anglophone markets.
- **Attribution risk:** a small number of quotes were pinned by exact-phrase re-search; those that couldn't be pinned were excluded rather than guessed — but snippet re-rendering risk remains on every verbatim.

## 2. Method-layer limitations

- **No frequencies were manufactured, which means no prevalence claims exist:** "recurring review theme" = ≥3 independent rows, nothing more. Where a number appears (complaint counts, tap counts, percentages), it is the *source's* count with the source's method, unverified.
- **Selection bias is structural:** review platforms over-represent the angry and the delighted; complaint registries only the angry; vendor blogs the flattering. The corpus triangulates across source types but cannot cancel this.
- **Vendor/competitor conflict-of-interest rows are flagged in-table** but could not be audited past the snippet.
- **Older threads mix with current ones** (some streak-grief threads likely pre-2020): treated as evidence of persistent patterns, not current events, wherever undated.
- **Synthetic materials are a separate universe:** the [synthetic stress test](synthetic-stress-test.md) and its derivatives are labelled hypothesis-generation and appear nowhere in the corpus or this evidence set's claims (R-19 guards the boundary).

## 3. Q15 — What public evidence cannot responsibly answer *(the honest list, feeding D-009 escalation design)*

1. **Relative demand sizing between goal categories** — no obtainable search-volume/thread-count data; our category-demand vote exists precisely because this is unanswerable from public sources.
2. **Willingness-to-pay at our price points** — public evidence shows *mechanisms* of paying and refusing, never our conversion; only the pricing-interest test (weak, honest) and Gate-D transactions (real) answer it.
3. **The 12-week frame's motivational valence for our audience** — trainer-side rationale exists; user-side preference between 8/12-week frames is thin; passive arms + possible escalation answer it.
4. **Week-13 multi-cycle appetite** — invisible to public data entirely; only real cohorts show it.
5. **Our product's comprehension, usability and age-band performance** — no amount of category evidence substitutes for Stage-5 testing with our own artefact, especially for the 65+ cohort (and unmoderated methods cannot *diagnose* those failures — moderated escalation exists for exactly that).
6. **Safety-sensitive behaviour** (e.g., whether deadline-driven users would misuse a programme) — public evidence suggests the risk class; individual behaviour requires governance design plus real-cohort observation, never assumption.
7. **Older-user prevalence within named competitor apps** — self-identified-age reviews were unretrievable; the systemic-exclusion finding rests on surveys/research, not per-app counts.
8. **Emotional response to our recovery copy** — the forgiveness-is-rewarded precedent (T12) is the nearest neighbour; our specific copy's reception is testable only with people (word-sort protocols staged).
9. **Lifetime-purchase regret** and **quarterly-billing-as-positive** user voice — genuinely unretrievable this pass; noted for any future access-improved sweep.
10. **Anything about causation** — the corpus is observational voice; it locates hypotheses and vocabulary, never proves mechanisms.
