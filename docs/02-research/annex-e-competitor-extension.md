## Category 1 — Guided fitness

### 1.1 Centr (Chris Hemsworth)

**FACT — Positioning/promise:** All-in-one fitness/wellness platform "inspired by Chris Hemsworth" — workouts, meal plans, mindfulness, delivered by Hemsworth's team of trainers/chefs/mindfulness guides (Centr homepage — https://centr.com/ — 2026-07-26; TechRadar review — https://www.techradar.com/health-fitness/fitness-apps/centr-review — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Audience:** general-consumer, home-and-gym exercisers wanting a holistic (training+food+mind) plan; beginner→advanced levels selectable at onboarding (Reviewed — https://www.reviewed.com/health/content/centr-chris-hemsworth-app-review — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Onboarding:** brief quiz — body stats (height/weight), fitness level (beginner/intermediate/advanced), goal (lose weight / build muscle / get fit and toned), dietary preference (meat-eater/pescatarian/vegetarian/vegan) — roughly 4–6 inputs; then membership selection with 7-day free trial (payment details up front; memberships 1/3/12 months) before content access (Reviewed, ibid.; Apple App Store listing — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/centr-strength-fitness-app/id1382530817 — 2026-07-26). **[INT]** Paywall sits immediately after the quiz, before any first-session value.
**FACT — Daily experience:** planner/calendar prescribes one workout, one meditation and three meals per day; recipes auto-populate a shopping list; library ~1,000 workouts, ~493 recipes, ~137 mindfulness sessions (Reviewed, ibid.; Fit&Well — https://www.fitandwell.com/reviews/centr-review — 2026-07-26). High guidance, low required logging **[INT]**.
**Recovery after missed days:** not documented in sources found (see could-not-verify).
**FACT — Progress model:** program-based training plans plus the daily planner; App Store rating ~4.7 from 17,000+ reviews (GymBird review — https://www.gymbird.com/fitness-apps/centr-app-review — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Monetisation/pricing (conflicting — range):** $29.99/mo and $149.99/yr (2026 search consensus incl. Cora comparison — https://www.corahealth.app/compare/centr — 2026-07-26); one App-Store-indexed snippet cites "$20/month" (apps.apple.com, ibid.); older reviews cite $119.99/yr. Approx £23/mo; £94–£117/yr. Sources conflict; treat as $120–$150/yr.
**FACT — Strength:** breadth/production quality of the holistic offer — "a genuinely useful one-stop wellness hub" (TechRadar, ibid.).
**FACT — Complaint:** Trustpilot ~1.5/5 from ~109 reviews; dominant theme is billing — charges continuing after cancellation, auto-billing of the most expensive plan, refusal of refunds (Trustpilot — https://www.trustpilot.com/review/centr.com — 2026-07-26).
**Accessibility:** nothing verifiable found.
**Design identity [INT]:** premium celebrity-fronted glossy video production — aspirational Hollywood-body branding over a planner chassis.
**Lesson [INT]:** the "one workout + one meditation + three meals laid out for today" planner is the strongest guidance-first pattern in fitness — a 12-week app should own the *day view* the same way.
**Do not copy [INT]:** card-first trial with hostile cancellation/refund handling — it has bought Centr a 1.5-star trust profile that contradicts the brand.

### 1.2 Nike Training Club (free model)

**FACT — Positioning/promise:** free workout library + multi-week programs from Nike trainers; premium tier abolished — made free for 90 days in March 2020 ("Play inside, play for the world"), then permanently free from July 2020 (MediaPost — https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/348941/ — 2026-07-26; Nasdaq — https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/nike-moves-to-make-the-nike-training-club-app-free-permanently-2020-07-15 — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Audience:** mass-market, all levels; ~500 on-demand workouts across strength, HIIT, yoga, pilates, mindfulness (Reviewed — https://www.reviewed.com/health/content/nike-training-club-review-workout-app — 2026-07-26; Fitness Tools Reviewed — https://fitnesstoolsreviewed.com/app-reviews/nike-training-club-review-is-the-free-app-worth-it/ — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Onboarding:** create free Nike account; short quiz on workout preferences/frequency; choosing a program adds a brief fitness-level questionnaire; no payment details ever requested (Fitness Tools Reviewed, ibid.; onboarding teardown — Medium/Stephanie Sutanto — https://medium.com/@stephsutanto/nike-training-club-app-user-onboarding-teardown-66d72e29e9c9 — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Daily experience:** self-serve library plus structured 4–6-week programs (25–40-min sessions ramping in intensity); audio/video instructor guidance; no human interaction (MakeUseOf — https://www.makeuseof.com/how-use-nike-training-club-app-next-level-fitness/ — 2026-07-26; Garage Gym Reviews — https://www.garagegymreviews.com/equipment/nike-training-club — 2026-07-26).
**Recovery:** no penalty mechanics documented; library model — you simply resume **[INT]**.
**FACT — Progress model:** Activity/Achievements views — trophies, milestones, streaks for workout counts and weekly frequency (MakeUseOf, ibid.).
**FACT — Monetisation:** none in-app; free with no tiers — the app is a brand/commerce funnel (Fitness Tools Reviewed, ibid.). £0.
**FACT — Strength:** entire high-production library free with no payment wall — repeatedly rated best free workout app (Tom's Guide — https://www.tomsguide.com/reviews/nike-training-club-app — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Complaint:** "no customization, support, or interaction from any of the coaches" (Garage Gym Reviews, ibid.); a design critique also calls the trophy system overbearing "featuritis" (Pratt IXD — https://ixd.prattsi.org/2023/09/design-critique-nike-training-club-iphone-app/ — 2026-07-26); repeated benchmark sessions reuse identical trainer audio, undermining the sense of progress (Medium/Jorge Mir — https://medium.com/@jmiralva/nike-training-d18674a31df1 — 2026-07-26).
**Accessibility:** audio-guided workouts exist (workouts instructed via audio + demo video) (Reviewed, ibid.); no formal a11y documentation found.
**Design identity [INT]:** athletic, monochrome, big-type Nike brand confidence; content-catalogue first, coaching second.
**Lesson [INT]:** free-forever bought NTC unmatched goodwill — but with zero personalisation it converts poorly into *transformation*; guidance and adaptation, not content volume, is the defensible layer for a paid 12-week product.
**Do not copy [INT]:** achievement/trophy spam decoupled from the user's actual goal, and "progress" that doesn't acknowledge the user's history (same audio on repeat benchmarks).

### 1.3 Couch to 5K — NHS version + independent (Zen Labs C25K)

**FACT — Positioning/promise (NHS):** free, government-backed app taking "complete non-runners to running 5K in 9 weeks"; built by PHE/DHSC with BBC Sport, launched 2016; formerly branded "One You Couch to 5K", now "NHS Couch to 5K" under Better Health (BBC — https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/uk-53242779 — 2026-07-26; gov.uk press releases — https://www.gov.uk/government/news/record-numbers-complete-nhs-couch-to-5k-app and https://www.gov.uk/government/news/millions-of-runs-completed-using-couch-to-5k-app — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Traction:** 7M+ downloads since 2016; 858,000 downloads March–June 2020 (+92% YoY); 8.7M runs completed and 790k downloads in 2024 alone (gov.uk, ibid.; BBC, ibid.).
**FACT — Onboarding:** minimal — pick one of five celebrity coach voices (Michael Johnson, Jo Whiley, Sarah Millican, Sanjeev Kohli, Jennie Bond), set reminders, start Week 1 Run 1; no paywall, ever; no quiz (Motera review — https://www.motera.app/nhs-couch-to-5k-app — 2026-07-26; Tom's Guide — https://tomsguide.com/reviews/nhs-couch-to-5k-app-review — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Daily/weekly experience:** 3 coached runs/week × 9 weeks (27 sessions); every session = 5-min warm-up walk, voice-coached run/walk intervals, 5-min cool-down; time-based not distance-based in early weeks so no pace monitoring needed (NICS Well week-by-week — https://www.nicswell.co.uk/live-well/couch-to-5k-week-by-week — 2026-07-26; findyouredge guide — https://www.findyouredge.app/news/couch-to-5k-complete-uk-beginner-guide-2026 — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Recovery after missed days/failed runs (documented):** the design explicitly permits repeating any run or week until ready; most users finish in 9–12+ weeks rather than 9 (NICS Well, ibid.; Motera, ibid.). This is the clearest documented "recovery" model in this annex.
**FACT — Progress model:** linear week/run completion; graduation at Week 9.
**FACT — Monetisation:** none. £0 (Google Play listing — https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.phe.couchto5K — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Strength:** the most trusted beginner running plan in the UK; free, simple, coach-voice-led (Tom's Guide, ibid.).
**FACT — Complaint:** store/community reports of getting stuck at coach-selection during setup with no skip; no music integration and audio-ducking/timer clashes with other apps; data loss when switching devices (HealthUnlocked threads — https://healthunlocked.com/couchto5k/posts/140584670/app-problems — 2026-07-26; Motera, ibid.).
**FACT — Accessibility:** fully audio-guided (usable without looking at screen); time-based progression removes pace/distance tech demands; DHSC publishes a support contact (digitalproducts@dhsc.gov.uk) (Motera, ibid.; HealthUnlocked, ibid.). Formal accessibility statement: not surfaced in search.
**Independent version (Zen Labs C25K) — FACT:** free with ads; IAPs: Remove Ads $1.99 (£1.55 approx), "Go To Pro" $4.99 (£3.90), $29.99 unlock, Zen Unlimited Pass $74.99/yr (£59 approx); heavily media-cited legacy app; complaint: "unless you pay for ZenLabs Unlimited, features are extremely basic, no Apple Watch integration" (Apple App Store — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/c25k-5k-trainer/id485971733 — 2026-07-26; AppGrooves review aggregation — https://appgrooves.com/app/c25k-5k-trainer-couch-potato-to-running-5k-by-zen-labs-llc-ca-1/negative — 2026-07-26).
**Design identity [INT]:** NHS: institutional, warm, deliberately untechnical; Zen Labs: dated utility app wrapped in upsells.
**Lesson [INT]:** C25K is the best proof that a fixed-length, voice-guided, interval-structured programme with *sanctioned repetition* ("repeat a week — it's normal") completes at scale. "12 Weeks" should make repeating/rescheduling a first-class, shame-free mechanic exactly like this.
**Do not copy [INT]:** NHS's zero-flexibility setup bugs (blocked at coach choice) and lack of music/device sync; Zen Labs' ad-cluttered freemium that paywalls basics of a free protocol.

## Category 2 — Skill learning

### 2.1 Mimo (learn to code)

**FACT — Positioning:** Duolingo-style bite-sized coding lessons (Python, JS, HTML/CSS etc.) with free Basic tier; "learn to code" mission (mimo.org — https://mimo.org/ — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Onboarding/paywall:** goal/experience quiz, then paywall appears early in onboarding with a 14-day free trial offer; free path continues underneath (ScreensDesign teardown — https://screensdesign.com/showcase/mimo-learn-codingprogramming — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Daily experience:** short interactive lessons, hearts/ads on free tier, projects and certificates on Pro; Max adds career paths, guided projects, AI tutor, web access (Mimo support — https://support.mimo.org/hc/en-us/articles/12752187831314 and https://support.mimo.org/hc/en-us/articles/14951451385746 — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Recovery (documented):** Streak Freeze purchasable in advance (auto-activates on a missed day); Streak Repair item recovers the last missed day, usable once per month (Mimo support — https://support.mimo.org/hc/en-us/articles/360017480999 and https://support.mimo.org/hc/en-us/articles/360017480779 — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Progress model:** streaks, in-app currency/store, certificates, career paths (support pages, ibid.).
**FACT — Pricing (approximate, conflicting):** Pro ≈ $10–15/mo billed yearly (~$100/yr; user reports "$100" charges; one UK Trustpilot reviewer ~£50/yr on promo); Max materially higher (AUD figures ~52 vs ~17 seen in reviews). Official page prices not indexable. Approx £78/yr Pro, range £50–£100 (Coddy comparison — https://coddy.tech/vs/mimo — 2026-07-26; App Store reviews — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mimo-learn-coding-programming/id1133960732?see-all=reviews — 2026-07-26; Trustpilot — https://www.trustpilot.com/review/mimo.org — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Strength:** consistently praised bite-sized lesson design that keeps daily practice going (Trustpilot positive themes, ibid., 826 reviews).
**FACT — Complaint:** Pro buyers pushed to upgrade again to Max for features they thought they'd bought ("making the Pro version feel like the free one"), plus refusal of refunds hours after unwanted annual charges (Trustpilot, ibid.).
**Accessibility:** nothing found.
**Design identity [INT]:** friendly gamified dark-IDE aesthetic — Duolingo's grammar applied to code.
**Lesson [INT]:** paid recovery items aside, Mimo's *pre-purchasable* streak freeze is a good pattern: let users plan for absence in advance rather than only repair after failure.
**Do not copy [INT]:** re-segmenting the paid tier after purchase (Pro→Max bait feeling) — fatal for a trust-dependent 12-week commitment product.

### 2.2 Yousician (instrument)

**FACT — Positioning:** gamified instrument learning (guitar, piano, bass, uke, singing) with real-time mic feedback on your actual playing (yousician.com — https://yousician.com/ — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Onboarding/paywall:** choose instrument, play-along level assessment; free tier limited to ~15 min/day or one lesson/day, so the effective paywall arrives after the first session; 7-day premium trial exists (TheGuitarLesson review — https://www.theguitarlesson.com/guitar-lesson-blog/guitar-lesson-review/yousician-review/ — 2026-07-26; Yousician support — https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336137 — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Daily experience:** falling-note gameplay, instant accuracy feedback, structured levels; high engagement, moderate input (you play, it listens) (American Songwriter review — https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-review/ — 2026-07-26).
**Recovery:** no documented missed-day mechanics found.
**FACT — Pricing (conflicting — range):** Premium $9.99/mo (one instrument); Premium+ $19.99/mo or $179.99/yr (all instruments + popular songs); Black-Friday half-price offers imply a $139.99/yr list at times. Approx £7.80/mo; £109–£140/yr (Guitar Chalk — https://www.guitarchalk.com/yousician-cost/ — 2026-07-26; Subger — https://subger.com/en/service/yousician — 2026-07-26; Slickdeals — https://slickdeals.net/f/16269802-yousician-bf-pricing-get-premium-for-50-off-69-99-ends-today — 2026-07-26). Sources conflict.
**FACT — Strength:** real-time feedback on a real instrument — the core differentiator reviewers credit for beginner progress (American Songwriter, ibid.).
**FACT — Complaint:** billing — "annual subscription paid monthly" lock-in that users can't cancel penalty-free; charges after cancellation; PissedConsumer 1.2/5 (Trustpilot — https://www.trustpilot.com/review/yousician.com — 2026-07-26; PissedConsumer — https://yousician.pissedconsumer.com/review.html — 2026-07-26). Also: adults find the gamification "infantilizing" (MasterPiano review — https://www.masterpiano.com/yousician-review — 2026-07-26).
**Accessibility:** audio-interactive by nature; no formal notes found.
**Design identity [INT]:** neon game-arcade meets music tuition; progress bars everywhere.
**Lesson [INT]:** assessment-by-doing (play and be placed) is the least painful onboarding "quiz" imaginable — 12 Weeks should prefer observed behaviour over questionnaires where possible.
**Do not copy [INT]:** annual-commitment-disguised-as-monthly billing — the single most repeated complaint; and tone that patronises adult learners.

### 2.3 Brilliant (STEM)

**FACT — Positioning:** interactive learn-by-doing STEM (maths, CS, data, science) courses; "daily lessons" habit frame (Brilliant FAQ — https://brilliant.org/faq/ — 2026-07-26; e-student review — https://e-student.org/brilliant-org-review/ — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Onboarding:** multi-step profile quiz — why you're here (school/professional/curiosity), comfort level per topic (introductory→advanced), topic selection — then trial/paywall offer; free tier = limited daily lessons via "keys"; 7-day premium trial, reportedly only startable within ~2 days of registration (UpskillWise — https://upskillwise.com/reviews/brilliant/ — 2026-07-26; Nibble — https://nibble-app.com/blog/is-brilliant-free — 2026-07-26; missiongraduatenm.org — https://missiongraduatenm.org/brilliant-free-trial/ — 2026-07-26 — lower-confidence source).
**FACT — Daily experience:** one-or-more short interactive problem-solving lessons; streak + daily challenges as the habit engine (e-student, ibid.).
**Recovery:** streak mechanics exist; no repair/freeze documentation found.
**FACT — Pricing (conflicting — range):** $27.99/mo or $161.88/yr ($13.49/mo equivalent) per 2026 sources; earlier guides cite $24.99/mo / $149.88/yr. Approx £22/mo, £117–£126/yr (e-student, ibid.; MyELearningWorld — https://myelearningworld.com/brilliant-pricing/ — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Strength:** genuinely interactive problem-first pedagogy, widely praised vs video courses (UpskillWise, ibid.).
**FACT — Complaint:** #1 reported issue is surprise auto-renewal — waking up to an unexpected ~$160–186 annual charge with a ~3-day refund window; secondary critique: knowledge feels superficial/doesn't transfer (YourAppLand — https://yourappland.com/is-brilliant-app-worth-it/ — 2026-07-26; Nibble — https://nibble-app.com/blog/is-brilliant-worth-it — 2026-07-26).
**Accessibility:** nothing found.
**Design identity [INT]:** clean, playful editorial-science illustration; feels like an interactive textbook, not a game.
**Lesson [INT]:** Brilliant converts a vague aspiration ("get smarter") into a *daily 5-minute concrete action* — the same move a 12-week goal app must make on day one.
**Do not copy [INT]:** silent annual renewal with a punitive refund window and trial-expiry tricks; it is their dominant negative-review theme.

## Category 3 — Goal planning / execution

### 3.1 Strides (goal + habit tracker)

**FACT — Positioning:** iOS-only SMART-goal and habit tracker; four tracker types (habit/target/average/project), 150+ templates (crm.org review — https://crm.org/news/strides-app-review — 2026-07-26; App Store — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/strides-habit-tracker-goals/id672401817 — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Audience:** self-directed organised iOS users; 4.8 App Store rating (AppleInsider — https://appleinsider.com/articles/23/02/02/strides-1521-review-visual-habit-tracking-at-its-best — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Onboarding/paywall:** no diagnostic quiz — pick/create trackers immediately; free up to 3 trackers; paywall triggers on the 4th tracker or advanced analytics (HabitBox — https://habitbox.app/blog/strides-app-review — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Daily experience:** pure self-logging with reminders and charts; zero guidance/coaching **[INT on the last clause]** (crm.org, ibid.).
**Recovery:** flexible targets/averages tolerate off days by design; no explicit missed-day feature documented.
**FACT — Progress model:** streaks, success rates, pace-vs-target ("on track" projections for target goals) charts (crm.org, ibid.).
**FACT — Pricing:** Strides Plus $4.99/mo, $39.99/yr, or $79.99 lifetime (approx £3.90/mo, £31/yr, £62 lifetime) (crm.org, ibid.; dailyhabits.xyz — https://www.dailyhabits.xyz/habit-tracker-app/strides — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Strength:** flexibility of tracker types with an uncluttered UI — "stellar at balancing an abundance of features without chaotic complexity" (AppleInsider via mobileappdaily roundup — https://www.mobileappdaily.com/product-review/strides-habit-tracker-app — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Complaint:** no Android after years of "coming soon", plus occasional sync bugs (Calmevo — https://calmevo.com/strides-app-review/ — 2026-07-26; HabitBox, ibid.).
**Accessibility:** nothing found.
**Design identity [INT]:** iOS-native, chart-heavy, quietly corporate blue — a spreadsheet with good manners.
**Lesson [INT]:** the target/average/project trio shows real goals aren't all daily habits; a 12-week app needs at least "pace toward a quantity by week 12" alongside daily actions.
**Do not copy [INT]:** guidance vacuum — Strides assumes users already know what to do, which is exactly the user "12 Weeks" must not assume; also single-platform strategy.

### 3.2 GoalsWon (goal coach with traction — chosen "credible goal coach app")

**FACT — Positioning:** 1-on-1 daily human accountability coaching in an app: set today's goals each morning, submit results at night, coach replies with personalised feedback within hours; monthly video call included (GoalsWon reviews page — https://www.goalswon.com/reviews — 2026-07-26; Graham Mann review — https://grahammann.net/blog/goalswon-review — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Audience/traction:** professionals/self-improvers; modest scale — Trustpilot 4/5 from ~42 reviews; iOS + Android apps exist (Trustpilot — https://www.trustpilot.com/review/goalswon.com — 2026-07-26; App Store — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/goalswon-accountability-coach/id1544973743 — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Onboarding:** set goals, get matched with a human coach; free trial before paying (TheBusinessDive — https://thebusinessdive.com/goalswon-review — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Recovery:** the mechanism *is* the recovery — you report what you didn't do and why; coach responds and re-plans **[INT framing]** (Graham Mann, ibid.).
**FACT — Pricing:** $99.99/mo, $269.99/quarter, $699.99/yr (approx £78/mo, £211/qtr, £547/yr) (TheBusinessDive, ibid.).
**FACT — Strength:** "it's the accountability of the coach that makes this so awesome" — human element repeatedly cited as the value (GoalsWon reviews, ibid.).
**FACT — Complaint (mild):** reviewers want richer goal views/longer-term progress visualisation in the app itself (Juliety — https://juliety.com/goalswon-review — 2026-07-26).
**Accessibility:** nothing found.
**Design identity [INT]:** utilitarian chat-plus-checklist; the product is the person, not the pixels.
**Lesson [INT]:** an evening "what happened and why" reflection reviewed by *someone* (human or AI) is the accountability core a guidance-first app can emulate at software prices.
**Do not copy [INT]:** thin in-app progress model — outsourcing all meaning to chat makes weekly/12-week arcs invisible.

## Category 4 — Behaviour change / coaching

### 4.1 Way of Life (legacy tracker — brief)

**FACT:** colour-coded yes/no/skip habit journal since ~2010-era iOS; seconds-per-day logging; chains + trend charts; free for 3 habits; premium: iOS listed at $4.99/mo (approx £3.90) while Android shows a $6.49 one-time unlock — sources conflict on model (Zapier roundup — https://zapier.com/blog/best-habit-tracker-app/ — 2026-07-26; Google Play — https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wayoflife.app — 2026-07-26; toolfinder — https://toolfinder.co/tools/way-of-life — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Strength:** the three-state answer ("did it / didn't / skipping") plus excellent data output (toolfinder, ibid.). **FACT — Complaint:** premium feels pricey for a simple tracker since the subscription shift (toggl roundup — https://toggl.com/blog/best-habit-tracker-apps — 2026-07-26).
**Recovery:** "skip" is a documented legitimate state — an early, elegant miss-handling design.
**Lesson [INT]:** distinguish "failed" from "legitimately skipped" — 12 Weeks should never paint a planned rest day red. **Do not copy [INT]:** retrofitting subscriptions onto a legacy one-time app — it reads as rent-seeking.

### 4.2 HabitBull (legacy tracker — brief)

**FACT:** iOS/Android habit tracker; month-grid view; flexible frequencies ("2× a week"); streak/percentage analytics; premium ~$4.99/mo or $19.99/yr (approx £15.60/yr); ratings ~4.7 iOS / 4.3 Android (dailyhabits.xyz — https://www.dailyhabits.xyz/habit-tracker-app/habitbull — 2026-07-26; HabitNoon — https://habitnoon.app/habit-tracker-app/habitbull — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Strength:** flexible scheduling + whole-month-at-a-glance grid (dailyhabits, ibid.). **Complaint:** no specific widely-reported complaint surfaced in searches — flagged in could-not-verify (its presence in 2025-26 roundups is mostly legacy listings **[INT]**).
**Lesson [INT]:** frequency-based habits ("3× this week") beat rigid daily chains for 12-week realism. **Do not copy [INT]:** analytics-dense grids as the *primary* surface — data view ≠ guidance.

### 4.3 Future (human coaching — key pricing anchor)

**FACT — Positioning:** remote 1-on-1 personal training: a real certified coach builds your weekly programme, monitors via Apple Watch (heart rate/completion), texts you daily, adjusts the plan as needed (Forbes Health — https://www.forbes.com/health/weight-loss/future-review/ — 2026-07-26; Healthline — https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/future-fitness-review — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Onboarding:** ~3-minute quiz (experience, goals, demographics, coach-style preference incl. "drill sergeant vs Zen"); app proposes 4 matched trainers with bios; payment taken after choosing coach; ~25-min video kickoff call; first workouts arrive about a week later (Billboard — https://www.billboard.com/culture/product-recommendations/future-training-app-sign-up-online-1236088267/ — 2026-07-26; Today — https://www.today.com/tmrw/i-tried-future-app-it-was-having-personal-trainer-my-t207503 — 2026-07-26). **[INT]** Paywall before any training value, but after a persuasive human-matching moment.
**FACT — Daily experience:** personalised audio-guided workouts with coach voice notes; morning texts from the coach; watch-verified completion (CNN Underscored — https://www.cnn.com/cnn-underscored/reviews/future-app — 2026-07-26).

## Category 6 — Accountability / commitment devices

### 6.1 StickK

**FACT — Positioning:** commitment-contract platform (web + iOS/Android): define goal + deadline, stake money, appoint a referee; failure sends stakes to a friend, charity, or an "anti-charity" you oppose; founded 2007-08 by Yale's Dean Karlan (economics), Ian Ayres (law) and Jordan Goldberg (Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StickK — 2026-07-26; TechCrunch 2008 — https://techcrunch.com/2008/02/15/stickk-allows-you-to-put-a-contract-on-yourself/ — 2026-07-26; stickK Help Center — https://stickk.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360025828514 — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Traction:** 437,000+ commitment contracts created (stickK About — https://www.stickk.com/aboutus — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Onboarding:** contract builder (goal, duration/reporting periods, stakes, referee, supporters) before any tracking begins; free to use — you only risk your own stakes (stickK FAQ — https://www.stickk.com/faq/charities/Commitment+Contracts and https://new.stickk.com/faq — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Daily/weekly experience & recovery:** self-report per reporting period; referee confirms or disputes; verification is honour-system and "easy to game" with a lenient referee (stickK FAQ Reporting — https://www.stickk.com/faq/reporting/Commitment+Contracts — 2026-07-26; Accountablo alternatives analysis — https://www.accountablo.com/blog/stickk-alternatives — 2026-07-26). A failed period forfeits that period's stake; contract continues (FAQ, ibid.).
**FACT — Monetisation:** platform free; anti-charities receive unsolicited donations (no partnership) (FAQ Charities, ibid.). £0 to use; stakes user-set.
**FACT — Strength:** behavioural-economics pedigree (loss aversion + anti-charity twist) that made commitment contracts mainstream (Wikipedia; Freakonomics — https://freakonomics.com/2008/01/stickk-to-your-commitments/ — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Complaint:** Trustpilot 2.1/5 "Poor" — login failures on app and web, servers offline blocking report submission while charges still processed, unresponsive support (Trustpilot — https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.stickk.com — 2026-07-26).
**Accessibility:** nothing found.
**Design identity [INT]:** 2008-vintage web utility that never grew a modern product around its big idea.
**Lesson [INT]:** stakes work best as an *optional sharpener* on top of a plan — and self-report + chosen referee is socially realistic verification for a consumer app. **Do not copy [INT]:** letting infrastructure rot while money is on the line — reliability is existential when users are paying for their own failure.

### 6.2 Beeminder

**FACT — Positioning:** quantified-self commitment device: every goal is a graph with a "bright red line"; go off-track ("derail") and you pay your pledge; pledge ladder $0/$5/$10/$30/$90/$270/$810/$2,430/$7,290, minimum charge $5; pledge caps settable; changes take 7 days (Beeminder help — https://help.beeminder.com/article/19-how-much-does-beeminder-cost and https://help.beeminder.com/article/20-how-much-do-i-pledge-on-my-goals — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Recovery (documented, unusually humane):** on derail you get a "legitimacy check" email; charge processes after 24h; non-legit derailments refunded "no questions asked"; official philosophy "derailing is nailing it" (help — https://help.beeminder.com/article/17-what-happens-when-i-derail — 2026-07-26; Beeminder blog — https://blog.beeminder.com/defail/ — 2026-07-26; utf9k year-one review — https://utf9k.net/blog/lessons-beeminder-year-one/ — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Monetisation:** primarily derailment revenue; optional premium tiers (Infinibee / Bee Plus / Beemium) — pricing conflicts across sources and eras ($4→$8/mo Infinibee; Beemium $32→$40→$50, one source $64/mo); discounts for students/jobseekers/non-OECD/seniors (Beeminder blog — https://blog.beeminder.com/infinibee/ — 2026-07-26; help pricing article, ibid.; beeminder.com/premium — not directly fetchable). Approx £6–£50/mo depending on tier/source — flagged.
**FACT — Onboarding:** create a goal + data source (manual or autodata integrations), then the red line starts; day-to-day interaction is minimal once set up (Katzenfabrik review — http://katzenfabrik.cat/blog/beeminder — 2026-07-26).
**FACT — Strength:** genuinely effective for data-driven users; near-zero daily friction after setup (Katzenfabrik, ibid.; utf9k, ibid.).
**FACT — Complaint:** steep learning curve/UX complexity, and the "perverse incentive" critique — being motivated to pay the company via failure feels wrong to many (Beeminder's own blog engages it — https://blog.beeminder.com/perverse/ — 2026-07-26; forum thread "Beeminder is not in the business of helping users" — https://forum.beeminder.com/t/it-seems-to-me-like-beeminder-is-not-in-the-business-of-helping-users/12349 — 2026-07-26).
**Accessibility:** nothing found.
**Design identity [INT]:** proudly nerdy graph-first tool; the aesthetic *is* the yellow brick road chart.
**Lesson [INT]:** the legitimacy-check + generous-refund pattern shows how to run consequences without cruelty — any 12-week stakes/streak feature needs a human-graceful appeal path. **Do not copy [INT]:** revenue structurally dependent on user failure — corrosive positioning for a transformation brand — and expert-level setup complexity.

## Cross-cutting observations (all INTERPRETATION)

1. **Billing is the reputation killer in this space:** Centr (1.5★ Trustpilot), Yousician (annual-as-monthly), Brilliant (surprise renewals), Mimo (Pro→Max), Leap (can't cancel). A 12-week product has a natural fix none of them use: a commitment priced and framed as *one 12-week term* with an explicit end/renewal decision.
2. **The recovery patterns worth stealing** are documented in exactly four places: NHS C25K (repeat weeks are normal), Way of Life (skip ≠ fail), Mimo (pre-planned freezes), Beeminder (legitimacy check + refund grace). Nobody combines them.
3. **The pricing corridor:** £0 (NTC/NHS/Gymshark) → ~£30–£130/yr (trackers, learning apps) → ~£78/mo (GoalsWon) → ~£116–155/mo (Future). The gap between £130/yr software and £900+/yr humans is where a guidance-first "software that notices you" product sits.
