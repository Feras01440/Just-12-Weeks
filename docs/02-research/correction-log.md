## Process rule going forward

Any new quantitative or legal claim entering the documentation must arrive with: source, date, primary/secondary flag, and a one-line "what the source actually establishes". Claims that can't meet that bar are written as **Unvalidated hypothesis** or omitted. Store-listing and marketing copy additionally pass the claims policy (`06-content/claims-policy.md`).
