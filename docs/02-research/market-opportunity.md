# Market Opportunity

**Status:** Assessment, 26 July 2026. Figures carry source-audit corrections; secondary-sourced numbers (†) must be re-verified before external use. This is an **Evidence-supported inference** document — the opportunity is argued from verified observations, not asserted.

## 1. The market is large, growing, and UK-viable

- Health & fitness apps: record ~$4.5B global IAP revenue in 2025, +13% YoY (Sensor Tower)†; UK second-largest market at ~8% of 2024 category spend† — a GBP-first launch addresses a real domestic market, not a rounding error.
- Broader personal-development spend (all formats) sits in the tens of billions†, with app-format habit/goal products a fragmented slice whose own market reports disagree by an order of magnitude (Annex B §3 — treated as unreliable and excluded from planning).
- Skill-learning subscription apps demonstrate the adjacent categories monetise at comparable prices (Brilliant ~$160/yr†, Yousician ~$110–180/yr†, Duolingo's 12.5M paid subscribers†).

## 2. The demand signal for the specific product shape

Converging evidence that people want *bounded, guided transformation* rather than open-ended tracking:

1. **Fixed-duration formats keep working at scale:** NHS C25K — 7M+ downloads, government-published completion celebrations†; 75 Hard's ecosystem sustains multiple paid apps†; Gymshark 66 is that brand's most successful community initiative†; The Body Coach built a reported £24M business from a 90-day plan†.
2. **Humans selling exactly our promise command 10–20× software prices:** Future ($149–199/mo)†, GoalsWon (~$100/mo)†, Coach.me ($87+/mo)† — the willingness-to-pay for "someone decides my day, notices me, adapts my plan" is proven; only its *price point* is unsolved.
3. **The review corpus asks for it verbatim:** P1 ("told me exactly what to do") and T5 ("nothing noticed me") in [user-review-themes](user-review-themes.md).
4. **The 12-week frame has cultural anchoring** (*The 12 Week Year* bestseller ecosystem†) with no consumer-grade mobile execution (its official product is web-first, professional-priced†).

## 3. The gap, precisely stated *(Evidence-supported inference)*

> **Guided, fixed-duration, multi-domain transformation at software prices — with recovery designed in — is unoccupied.**

Bounding evidence from 31 products ([competitor audit](competitor-audit.md)): the guided middle is held by humans (unscalable prices) or single-domain programmes (C25K, Body Coach — no platform breadth); recovery-as-content exists nowhere; governance-visible content exists nowhere; the pricing corridor between ~£130/yr software and ~£900+/yr humans is empty of credible "software that notices you" offerings.

**Honesty about the gap's nature:** a gap can mean unmet demand *or* a graveyard. Two failure explanations must be taken seriously: (a) guided depth at software prices may be *economically* hard (content cost — R-03), which is an execution moat if solved and a trap if not; (b) generalist positioning may fail acquisition against vertical specialists (T-ASO risk; synthesis §7.8). The Stage-1 kill signals exist precisely to test (a)-demand before (a)-cost is sunk; single-category launch positioning (Q1) answers (b).

## 4. Why now

- **Regulatory tailwind for the honest:** DMCC enforcement (first eight investigations, Nov 2025†) and Apple's false-price/4.3(b) posture punish exactly the patterns incumbents run on (T1/T2) — compliance is becoming a competitive weapon for products built clean.
- **Category fatigue is legible:** streak-anxiety and billing rage saturate review corpora (T1/T3); "calm tech" positioning has mainstream vocabulary now.
- **Incumbent drift:** Noom pivoted to medication†; Sleep Cycle is diversifying B2B as consumer shrinks†; the tracker pole is commoditised free. The guided-consumer middle is being vacated, not contested.
- **Platform mechanics matured:** store-native intro offers, committed-monthly annual plans (non-US)†, and win-back-free honest billing are all now implementable without custom infrastructure.

## 5. Sizing the realistic prize *(planning arithmetic, not a forecast)*

Bottom-up, UK-first, using corrected benchmarks: a niche-respectable outcome (year 2–3) of 300k cumulative installs × 3% install→paid† blended × ~£35 realized first-year revenue per payer† ≈ £315k ARR-equivalent — a real business for a founder-led company, far below venture scale. The upside case runs through completion-driven word of mouth (P2), programme catalogue expansion (D-004), and term-pricing lifting realized revenue per payer above category medians. **These numbers exist to size effort honestly, not to promise outcomes** — Stage 1/9 evidence replaces them.

## 6. What would falsify this opportunity assessment

Stage-1 interviews failing the demand kill-signals; landing-test interest below floor; beta week-4 survival at category-typical (~single-digit) levels despite recovery design; content cost per quality programme proving >2× the Q9 budget envelope; or a funded entrant occupying "guided 12-week platform" positioning with real depth before our launch window. Each is watched in the [risk register](../10-roadmap/risk-register.md).
