# Market Gaps and Risks

**Status:** Assessment, 26 July 2026. The strategic map distilled from the 31-product audit: which gaps we occupy, which we deliberately don't, and the market-side risks that could close or poison them. Cross-referenced to the [risk register](../10-roadmap/risk-register.md).

## 1. Gaps we intend to occupy (ranked by defensibility)

| # | Gap | Evidence | Defensibility **[INT]** |
|---|-----|----------|--------------------------|
| G1 | **Recovery-as-content** — authored re-entry days, fresh-start weeks, restructure paths | Exists nowhere in 31 products; only 4 partial recovery patterns found (C25K, Way of Life, Mimo, Beeminder) | High — requires engine + authored content + brand courage; can't be bolted onto a tracker without becoming a different product |
| G2 | **Guided fixed-duration transformation at software prices** | The pricing corridor (£130/yr ↔ £900+/yr) is empty; humans prove the demand | Medium-high — the moat is content economics (engine makes programme #n cheaper); imitable by a funded team |
| G3 | **Governance-visible content** (named reviewer, credentials, citations, review dates in-product) | Zero products surface it | Medium — trivially imitable in form, expensive in substance (real reviewers, real citations); first-mover trust compounding |
| G4 | **Honest term pricing** (12-week unit; explicit renewal decision) | Nobody sells the quarter as the headline unit; billing rage is the category's top complaint theme | Medium — imitable, but incumbents' revenue models resist it (their annual-plan economics depend on inertia) |
| G5 | **The completion brand** ("actually finishing" — week-12 report with comeback story) | C25K's graduation pride shows the emotional payload; no general product owns it | High as a brand position once occupied — brands are slow to dislodge |
| G6 | **Genuine accessibility / age-inclusivity** | No formal accessibility documentation found across 31 products; hustle-tone excludes older adults | Medium — imitable, but D-001 makes us structurally committed while competitors retrofit |

## 2. Gaps we deliberately do NOT occupy *(and why)*

- **Community/social accountability** (the one retention lever with category evidence we're deferring): moderation cost + different product shape; revisit post-MVP with evidence (N-20).
- **Human coaching marketplace:** proven willingness-to-pay, unscalable operations for a solo founder (N-23); the adaptation layer captures a slice of "being noticed" at software cost instead.
- **AI-generated personal plans:** the market is flooding with them; our governance model is the *opposite* bet (accountable human authorship) — contrarian by design (N-10).
- **Gamified engagement economies:** evidence cuts both ways and the brand cost is total (N-24, T3).
- **Free-forever:** earns goodwill, not transformation businesses (NTC lesson); we are honest-paid, not free.

## 3. Market-side risks to the gaps

| # | Risk | Watch signal | Register |
|---|------|--------------|----------|
| M1 | **A funded entrant takes G2 first** (most plausible: a coaching brand productising downmarket, e.g. Future-style player launching software tier; or Duolingo-style operator entering "life goals") | App-store new-entrant sweeps in quarterly audit cadence; funding announcements in the space | R-04 adjacency; new entry → full-framework audit |
| M2 | **Platform capture of the category** (OS-level habit/goal features; Apple/Google health surfaces absorbing "programmes") | WWDC/IO announcements | Accepted risk — differentiation is content + governance, which platforms don't do |
| M3 | **The gap is a graveyard** — guided depth at software price is economically unsound (content cost kills margin) | Authoring pilot actuals vs Q9 budget; programme #2 cost curve | R-03; Stage-2 authoring pilot before full build |
| M4 | **Generalist ASO failure** — vertical specialists out-acquire a platform story | Store-listing conversion tests per positioning (Stage 1/5) | Synthesis §7.8; Q1 single-category launch positioning |
| M5 | **Regulatory tide rises further** (DMCC subscription regime spring 2027; EU Digital Fairness Act naming countdown timers) | Legal watch, quarterly | Net *positive* for us if we stay clean — our compliance posture is a moat against incumbents built on T1 patterns |
| M6 | **Category reputation contagion** — users assume we bill/nag like the category does | Our own review corpus themes (T1/T3 appearing = messaging failure) | Paywall principles; launch copy leads with the honesty differences |
| M7 | **Seasonality dependence** (January/September concentration) | Cohort seasonality in beta/launch data | R-14; evergreen event-driven acquisition (Pattern C spillover) |

## 4. Positioning consequence *(Professional recommendation, consistent with D-004 and Q1's openness)*

Launch narrative leads with the **flagship programme's concrete promise** (vertical credibility, M4 mitigation) while the product surface quietly demonstrates the platform (catalogue + vote mechanic building the D-004 story and the programme-#2 demand evidence). The brand owns G5 ("finishing") from day one — it is the only gap that is simultaneously true of one programme and of the whole platform vision.
