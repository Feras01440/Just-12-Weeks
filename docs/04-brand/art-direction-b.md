# Art Direction B — "The Instrument" (Time and Momentum)

**Status:** Territory proposal for Gate 3 — one of three. Not selected. No screens designed.

## Strategic idea

Twelve weeks as **a precision instrument you wind and it holds**. The product as a beautiful measuring device — the lineage of chronographs, barometers, darkroom timers, lab balances: objects whose calm authority comes from doing one thing exactly. Where trackers nag, an instrument simply *shows true*. This is the territory closest to the founder's Sleep Cycle admiration — pursued as *warm instrument*, not clinical dashboard, and differentiated by the owned twelve-mechanic.

## Emotional character

Composed, exact, quietly powerful; the satisfaction of a mechanism engaging (the *click* of a week completing). Feels: dependable, adult, engineered-for-me. Never: cold, medical, dashboard-y.

## Visual metaphor & system

The dial and the mechanism. A twelve-position geometry (not a clock-face cliché — an abstract twelve-index bezel/ring) underlies the signature object: each week a position, each day a fine graduation; today is the active index. Surfaces as instrument faces: deep neutral grounds, precise graduation marks, one luminous indicator hue. Layout discipline from instrument panels: fixed reference positions (orientation always in the same place), high figure-ground clarity, zero decorative chrome.

## Typography philosophy

A precise **grotesk with engineered tabular numerals** as the single family (candidate space: Söhne, Unica-lineage, Neue Haas-lineage, IBM Plex Sans as open fallback — *to test, not chosen*; banned defaults excluded); numerals are the brand's letterforms (week numbers, graduations, durations) and must be exceptional at small sizes. Warmth enters through spacing, scale and the writing itself — the type stays cool so the voice can be warm (a deliberate tension that needs the voice rules enforced).

## Colour philosophy

Deep neutral instrument grounds (near-black warm greys; light mode as "daylight dial": warm off-white with dark graduations); **one luminous accent** in the phosphor/lume space (warm green-gold or signal orange candidates) reserved exclusively for *the live thing*: today's index, the active action, genuine milestones. Semantic colours conventional and separate. Programme accents as bezel tints per programme. Dark mode is this territory's native state; light mode designed with equal care (D-001 daylight readability).

## Image & illustration direction

Almost none — the instrument *is* the imagery. Demonstrations use clean video/photographic instruction in a neutral studio language; technical diagrams in graduation-mark style. No lifestyle photography anywhere.

## Motion philosophy

Mechanical honesty: movements that behave like mass and spring (short, damped, precise); the day completing = an index engaging (a felt *click*: haptic + a 120ms settle); the week completing = the ring advancing one position. Nothing floats, nothing bounces, nothing loops idly. Reduced-motion: state-steps with haptic/audio equivalents — an instrument with motion off is still an instrument (strongest reduced-motion story of the three).

## Iconography & data visualisation

Icons as engraved instrument markings: single-weight, geometric, unmistakable at 20pt. Dataviz as instrument readouts: graduation-marked scales, needle/index positions, trend as a drawn trace — numbers always adjacent in text (accessibility-native).

## Signature-experience fit

Strongest pairing: **The Twelve-Segment Object** (its definitive home) and the **Week-Turn** as mechanical engagement; the Accumulation Artefact renders as an engraved ring gaining graduations.

## Audience & commercial fit

- **D-001 fit: strong with care.** Instrument clarity (big figures, high contrast, fixed positions) is naturally elder-friendly; the risk is *coolness* reading as unwelcoming to low-digital-confidence users — mitigated by the voice and the ≤3-tap loop doing the warmth.
- **Category-neutral: strong** — a dial holds any goal; though the metaphor flatters measurable-progress programmes over soft ones (writing programme "graduations" need thought — the paper test for this territory).
- **Commercial:** premium-native; nearest to the founder's stated taste; risks adjacency to Sleep Cycle's look (mitigation: the twelve-mechanic and warmth are ours; and their category is sleep, not goals).

## Strengths / Risks

**Strengths:** most distinctive *motion/haptic* identity (the click is ownable); best reduced-motion story; tightest fit with "measurable" in the promise; executes superbly at implementation level (geometry, not media).
**Risks:** warm/cold balance is a knife-edge (the whole territory fails if it ships cold — voice QA is existential here); Sleep Cycle comparison invited; luminous-accent discipline erodes easily under feature pressure (guard: accent-use rules in the design system).

## Production implications

Medium: custom geometry/motion engineering (the ring must be flawless at 60fps and gorgeous frozen); minimal illustration; studio-standard demonstration media. Highest motion-engineering QA burden of the three.
