# Signature Visual Experience — Candidates

**Status:** Six candidates for Gate 3 evaluation. The signature is the owned mechanic that makes any screen recognisably ours (brand-strategy §8.1). Recommendation at the end proposes **which to prototype**, not which wins. Standing rules for all candidates: reduced-motion variants are designed equals; the mechanic must express semantic state to assistive tech ("Week 7 of 12 complete"); no candidate may celebrate ordinary days disproportionately; performance budget NFR-02 applies on low-end Android.

---

## S1 — The Twelve-Segment Object
A twelve-position geometric object (ring/bezel/seal per territory) that lives on Today and Journey: weeks as positions, days as fine graduations within the active position; completion advances the object; week 12 completes the form.
- **User meaning:** where am I, at a glance, always. **Emotional value:** steady accumulation; the click of advance.
- **Originality:** medium — segment-progress exists (activity rings); *twelve-as-identity* + graduation detail + cross-surface persistence is the ownable part.
- **Performance:** trivial (vector geometry). **Accessibility:** excellent — pure semantic state; text twin built in. **Reduced-motion:** stepped states, fully equal. **Android:** flawless.
- **Cost:** low. **Flexibility:** highest — renders in any territory's language (spine bands / bezel / route-stage ring); scales from app icon to report cover.

## S2 — The Bound Volume
The journey as a book being made: each week a chapter that visibly closes (a signature gathering, a spine band added); the shelf of finished volumes = the alumni identity (W3); the week-12 report *is* the finished book.
- **Meaning:** my twelve weeks become a made thing that persists. **Emotion:** authorship, permanence — strongest week-12 payoff of any candidate.
- **Originality:** high — no competitor product treats completion as artefact-making.
- **Performance:** light (2D layered). **Accessibility:** good (state = chapters closed; needs careful non-visual narration of the *making* quality). **Reduced-motion:** the closed-chapter state is inherently still. **Android:** fine.
- **Cost:** medium (the volume must be genuinely beautiful or the metaphor embarrasses). **Flexibility:** native to Territory A; adaptable elsewhere as "the record" but loses magic outside editorial language.

## S3 — The Route Line
One continuous drawn path across twelve terrains, threading Today (current leg), Journey (the whole route), recovery (the path resuming after weather), and week 12 (the route seen whole from above).
- **Meaning:** distance actually crossed; lapses as terrain, not holes — **the only candidate that makes recovery beautiful by default.**
- **Emotion:** earned distance; the resumption moment ("the line continues") is a potential brand-defining beat.
- **Originality:** high in execution (map-craft), medium in concept (journey lines exist in onboarding illustrations everywhere — ours is *functional*, not decorative).
- **Performance:** moderate (path rendering + terrain textures; needs discipline on low-end). **Accessibility:** good with work (the line's meaning must be fully text-mirrored; terrain textures are decoration-classed). **Reduced-motion:** pre-drawn states; the week-12 reveal as designed panorama. **Android:** needs perf care.
- **Cost:** medium-high (path engine + terrain system). **Flexibility:** native to C; portable as abstract progress-line elsewhere at reduced power.

## S4 — The Week-Turn
Not an object but a **ritual**: a designed 4–6 second weekly transition (chapter close / mechanical advance / camp move, per territory) marking the week boundary with the week's honest summary woven in — the product's version of a ceremony, once every seven days.
- **Meaning:** weeks have edges; passage is felt, and the summary makes it *informative* ceremony.
- **Emotion:** rhythm; the Sunday-evening quality the weekly review already owns, given a body.
- **Originality:** high — apps animate *actions*, almost none design *temporal ritual*.
- **Performance:** contained (one moment, prefetchable). **Accessibility:** must be skippable, fully narrated, never gate the review. **Reduced-motion:** a composed still sequence with the same information (designed, equal). **Android:** fine.
- **Cost:** low-medium per territory. **Flexibility:** total — it composes *with* S1/S2/S3 rather than competing (the turn is *how* the object advances).

## S5 — The Accumulation Artefact
A physical-logic object gaining material daily: layered print blocks (A) / engraved graduations (B) / cairn stones (C). Days add matter; nothing is ever removed (lapse = no addition, never subtraction — D-003-spirited honesty in object form).
- **Meaning:** what you built, made visible and *irreversible* — the anti-streak.
- **Emotion:** quiet compounding; deeply calm.
- **Originality:** medium-high (Forest's trees are kin; the never-subtracts law + adult materiality differentiates).
- **Performance:** light-moderate. **Accessibility:** good (count + description). **Reduced-motion:** additive states are naturally still. **Android:** fine.
- **Cost:** medium (84 states must stay beautiful). **Flexibility:** high across territories; risks *cuteness drift* in the wrong hands (guard: materiality rules, no faces, no garden).

## S6 — The Evidence Plates
Week-12's before-to-after presented as formal *paired plates* (museum-catalogue register): the goal statement, week-1 capability, week-12 capability, the user's chosen evidence — composed as a keepable diptych; the share card's engine.
- **Meaning:** honest transformation, formally witnessed. **Emotion:** proof; gravity.
- **Originality:** high in register (the category does before/after as gym-selfie; formal plate treatment is ours) — and it *only works* because claims-policy honesty backs it.
- **Performance:** static composition. **Accessibility:** fully text-structured. **Reduced-motion:** n/a (still by nature). **Android:** fine.
- **Cost:** low-medium. **Flexibility:** all territories; scope-limited (a completion-moment signature, not an everyday one).

## Prototyping recommendation *(Professional recommendation — for Gate 3, with the territory choice)*

Prototype **three**: **S1 + S4 as a composed pair** (the persistent object + the ritual that advances it — cheap, universal, and together they already constitute a signature system), and **the chosen territory's native hero** (A→S2, B→S1-as-hero deepened, C→S3). **S6 is recommended for adoption regardless of territory** (completion-moment signature; low cost, on-promise, feeds the share loop). S5 is held in reserve — adopt only if the chosen territory's hero tests weak. Evaluation in prototype: recognisability (masked-screenshot test — "whose app is this?"), comprehension across age cohorts, reduced-motion parity review, low-end-Android frame audit.
