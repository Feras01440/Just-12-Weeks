# Anti-Generic Design Audit

**Status:** Standing instrument — used at every design review, every release, and on every store asset, from Stage 3 onward. Purpose: detect AI-template convergence and category mimicry before users do. Apple's revised 4.3(b) (June 2026) makes this a review-survival tool, not just taste.

## The governing question (asked of every major screen, answered in writing at design review)

> **"What makes this screen recognisably part of this product rather than any other habit app?"**

A valid answer names something *owned*: the signature object/mechanic, the typographic system, the voice, the recovery register, the governance surface. "Our colours" alone is not a valid answer. No valid answer → the screen goes back.

## Surface checklists

**Typography** — ☐ Brand face per typography-strategy (not Inter/Poppins/Manrope/Montserrat/SF-Pro-as-brand) ☐ Scale shows deliberate hierarchy, not uniform 16px soup ☐ Tabular numerals in data positions ☐ 200% behaviour designed ☐ No faux-weight or tracking crimes.

**Colour** — ☐ Roles-first tokens, no ad-hoc hexes ☐ No purple-blue gradient, no teal-by-default ☐ Identity accent obeys the usage law (actions/live/milestones only) ☐ Both themes designed, ratios measured ☐ Nothing meaning-bearing by colour alone.

**Layout** — ☐ Not a card-grid-of-everything; cards only where containment means something ☐ One primary action per screen ☐ Structure follows the territory's system (baseline grid / instrument positions / route logic), not a template's ☐ Generous space used purposefully, not as emptiness cosplay.

**Navigation** — ☐ Three destinations, visible labels ☐ No drawer, no mystery-meat icons ☐ Platform idioms respected without breaking brand structure.

**Cards & containers** — ☐ No glassmorphism ☐ No elevation soup (shadows ration per design system) ☐ Radii from the token scale, not 24px-rounded-everything.

**Buttons & inputs** — ☐ States complete (incl. focus visible) ☐ Labels are verbs ☐ No gradient-pill defaults ☐ Touch targets per accessibility.

**Iconography** — ☐ Drawn to the territory's icon language, single metaphor per icon ☐ Not raw icon-library defaults (a library may seed, the redraw makes it ours) ☐ Meaning survives greyscale.

**Illustration** — ☐ Instructional purpose first ☐ Style-guide conformant ☐ Depicts adults across ages (D-001 representation rule) ☐ No Corporate-Memphis blobs, no random isometrics.

**Photography** — ☐ Only user evidence or instructional studio ☐ No stock aspiration (sunrise silhouettes, laughing salads, glistening abs) ☐ No before/after in marketing (claims policy).

**3D & effects** — ☐ No decorative 3D objects ☐ Any dimensionality earns meaning (⚠Q8: literal 3D remains unapproved) ☐ No particle confetti as personality.

**Motion** — ☐ Obeys the territory's motion philosophy (settle/mechanical/travel) ☐ Nothing bounces for charm ☐ Reduced-motion variant is a designed equal, reviewed alongside ☐ Celebration proportionality respected.

**Copywriting** — ☐ Voice table + lexicons pass ☐ No AI-flavoured filler ("Let's dive in!", "You've got this!", "Great job!") ☐ Specifics over adjectives ☐ The screen's words could not be pasted into a competitor unchanged.

**Empty states** — ☐ Authored with purpose (what this space becomes) ☐ No shrugging mascots, no "Nothing here yet 😊".

**Data visualisation** — ☐ Dataviz roles/ramps only ☐ Words carry the meaning alongside marks ☐ No decorative charts, no donut-for-the-sake-of-donut ☐ Honest axes (no truncated drama).

**Onboarding** — ☐ ≤3 value screens, skippable ☐ No permission ambush ☐ No carousel-of-features cliché — show the product's actual guidance instead.

**Paywall** — ☐ Paywall-principles checklist (its own constitution) ☐ Visually ours (the most template-prone screen in any app — extra scrutiny) ☐ G9 comprehension evidence attached.

**App icon** — ☐ Derived from the signature object ☐ Legible at 29pt ☐ Not: gradient rounded square with a tick/flame/leaf ☐ Tested in a folder full of habit apps for instant distinguishability.

**Store screenshots** — ☐ Real product states ☐ Claims-policy pass ☐ Territory system visible (a stranger could match screenshots to app in one glance) ☐ No floating-phone-on-gradient template.

## The mimicry sweep (quarterly + at every release)

Screenshot the product beside the four occupied aesthetic centres (gamified-cute / glossy-celebrity / utility-chart / institutional-warm) and current template marketplaces: any surface that could be traded into those decks unnoticed is logged as a defect with an owner and a fix date. Masked-logo test on five key screens with 5 external viewers: target ≥4/5 correct attribution to us by Stage 9.

## Enforcement

The audit runs: at every Gate-4/5 design review (per-screen written answers filed); pre-submission on all store assets (Stage 10 checklist); quarterly live. Failures are defects, triaged like functional bugs. The audit's findings feed the design system's rules — every failure becomes a codified prevention, so the instrument sharpens with use.
