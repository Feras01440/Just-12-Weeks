# Experience Direction Comparison

**Status:** decision-support, deliberately not a decision. Scores are the sprint team’s honest professional judgement after the critique passes (1–5; parentheses note the deciding evidence). Side-by-side visual evidence: `design-lab/reports/comparison-index.html`.

## The differentiation check (are these really three products?)

| Axis | A · Quarto | B · Meridian | C · Atelier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governing metaphor | a book being read | a field instrument | a cloth being woven |
| Composition | symmetric text column, margins | indexed face + graduated rails | asymmetric column + persistent band |
| Hierarchy engine | optical size & typographic register | engraving vs reading (caps/mono) | display grotesque vs text serif |
| Typography | Fraunces (one family, opsz 9–144) | Archivo (wdth axis) + IBM Plex Mono | Bricolage Grotesque + Faustina |
| Spatial rhythm | baseline & hairlines | 4px machine module & ticks | soft rows, material spacing |
| Navigation expression | pages & contents | indexed positions & log | rows, threads & the loom |
| Journey visualisation | table of contents, uncut pages | vertical rail with recorded readings | the loom: cloth + bare warp |
| Progress representation | pages read, folio arithmetic | readings recorded on a rail | material accumulated |
| Recovery | bookmark & revised edition | recalibration & capacity dial | visible mending & tie-back-on |
| Imagery | engraved plates, ornament | measured drawing | field-guide plates, woven texture |
| Motion | settle, ink, stamp | sweep, detent, press | draw, beat-up, unroll |
| Haptics concept | stamp confirm, chapter detents | rail detents, arm click | pick confirm, row-end tick |
| Emotional character | literary permanence | assured capability | patient making |
| Twelve | chapters I–XII | detents 01–12 | twelve woven rows |

No axis shares an expression across two directions; the recoloured-thrice failure mode is structurally excluded.

## What the scores do **not** say

They do not pick a winner. A wins on cost, calm and age-range confidence; B wins on interaction quality and world-neutrality; C wins on signature, artefact and emotional arc. These are different *bets*: A bets on restraint, B bets on competence, C bets on meaning.

## Hybridisation notes (post-decision options, recorded not chosen)

- The **capacity dial** (B) is metaphor-portable and could live inside A or C’s long-absence flow.
- The **counts-in-full** flag, the covenant copy, and the one-question pattern are already shared DNA — they survive any choice.
- A’s **uncut pages** concept (progressive revelation of future weeks) is portable to B (sealed detents — already implemented) and C (unstrung warp — already implemented); it should be preserved regardless.
- C’s completion-artefact thinking (the record as an object, unique per journey) should inform A/B artefacts even if C is not chosen.

## Elements safe to preserve regardless of flagship programme

One-action Today; the 33-state architecture; the recovery covenant and its three-tier flows; equal-status easier alternative; honest commerce states; text-paired progress representation; the motion doctrine (productive/expressive split, reduced-motion twins); the typography floors.

## Elements dependent on the flagship

The dye/accent mapping (C) and log vocabulary (B) per programme; imagery commissioning style; the voice register of instruction copy (a strength flagship pulls toward the manual, a writing flagship toward the almanac); week-naming conventions; the completion artefact’s content model.
