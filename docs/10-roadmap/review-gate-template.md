# Review Gate Template

**Status:** Active instrument. Every stage gate (milestone-gates.md) is decided from a **gate pack** built to this template. Design constraint (D-008): the founder must be able to decide honestly within **≤2 hours of reading in a 6–8 hour gate week** — the pack front-loads the decision and layers the evidence beneath it.

---

```
GATE PACK: Gate ___ (___ → ___)   ·   Date assembled:
Assembled by (product lead):          Checked by (independent reviewer):
Founder decision requested by: (date — pack delivered ≥72h before)

════ PAGE 1 — THE DECISION SHEET (one page, always) ════

1. THE QUESTION
What the founder is being asked to approve, in one sentence.

2. RECOMMENDATION
Proceed / proceed-with-conditions / redirect / stop — with the two-sentence why.

3. GATE CHECKLIST STATE
X of Y items green (full checklist in §B). Items not green, each with:
impact · fix plan · or waiver requested (risk named).

4. THE EVIDENCE THAT MATTERS (≤5 bullets)
The strongest findings for and against, each traceable to §C.

5. WHAT THIS APPROVAL UNLOCKS
Next stage's work · capital unlocked (staged-capital-plan gate + caps) ·
new tools/permissions · anything irreversible (flagged ⚠).

6. DECISIONS BUNDLED AT THIS GATE
Reserved decisions due now, each: question · options · recommendation · memo ref.

7. WHAT REMAINS OPEN (deliberately)
So silence is never read as approval of unlisted things.

════ SECTION B — CHECKLIST DETAIL ════
The milestone-gates checklist, item by item, with evidence links.

════ SECTION C — EVIDENCE APPENDIX ════
Source documents, dashboards, review verdicts, spend record vs caps,
risk-register deltas, correction-log entries since last gate.

════ FOUNDER RECORD (completed at decision) ════
Decision:            Conditions/waivers:
Notes:               Date:
→ transcribed to decision-log.md by product lead, checked by reviewer.
```

---

## Rules

1. **No ambush gates:** pack delivered ≥72 hours before a decision is requested; the founder may extend without penalty — stages have no clock pressure that beats decision quality (quality-gates-take-priority, D-007).
2. **No pack, no gate:** verbal/chat approvals of stage transitions are not valid; the record is the pack + decision-log entry.
3. **Checker before founder:** the independent reviewer signs the pack's honesty (checklist states real, evidence traceable, waivers not buried) before it reaches the founder.
4. **Waivers are loud:** any red item passed under waiver appears on Page 1 and in the decision log with its named accepted risk — never in a footnote.
5. **Stop is a first-class outcome:** packs present stop/redirect honestly when evidence points there; a gate pack that can only recommend "proceed" is broken by construction.
