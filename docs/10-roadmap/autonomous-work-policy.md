# Autonomous Work Policy

**Status:** Active — the boundary conditions under which implementers work without founder involvement (D-008). The default is autonomy; this page defines its edges precisely so autonomy stays safe rather than timid.

## 1. Always autonomous (no approval needed)

Research from public, lawful sources with the project's capture/classification discipline · documentation drafting and revision · analysis and synthesis · internal tooling and scaffolding · engineering work inside approved scope on non-production surfaces · testing of every kind against internal builds · register maintenance · preparation of decision memos, gate packs, experiments and materials **up to but not including publication**.

## 2. Never autonomous (founder-reserved, per delivery model §3)

Contacting users, participants or any external person on the project's behalf · publishing anything (landing pages, store listings, posts, ads) · spending beyond the current gate's caps (staged-capital-plan) or activating any £0-cap line · taking payments · founder-reserved decisions (name, brand, flagship, monetisation, launch, ADR approvals) · changing confirmed founder decisions or safety boundaries · touching production systems or real user data · engaging specialists/contractors · anything irreversible outside approved scope.

## 3. Standing behavioural rules

1. **Evidence honesty is absolute:** no fabricated participants, quotes, results, frequencies or credentials — ever, in any artefact, including drafts. Synthetic material is labelled synthetic at every appearance (Part-5 rule; a synthetic panel is never "user research").
2. **Maker–checker:** substantial work routes through an independent reviewer before it feeds a gate or register (delivery model §1).
3. **Escalate on contradiction:** evidence that contradicts a confirmed founder decision is escalated, not silently accommodated or buried.
4. **Scope discipline:** drift beyond a packet's out-of-scope line becomes a new packet; "while I was in there" is not authorisation.
5. **Attribution policy** (CONTRIBUTING.md) applies to every repository-facing artefact produced autonomously.
6. **Privacy floor:** the product's own data-handling standards (`08-security/*`, consent procedures) bind research and operations now, not just the future app.
7. **Time-boxing honesty:** work that can't meet its packet's window hands off cleanly (handoff standard §3.4) rather than expanding silently.
8. **Tool discipline:** only register-compatible tools/services; new tools enter the third-party register before use, even on free tiers.

## 4. Safe-harbour interpretation rule

If an action's classification is ambiguous, classify it one step more restrictive (routine→reversible, reversible→costly, costly→reserved) and act accordingly. The cost of a queued question is hours; the cost of a wrong irreversible act is the project's trust.

## 5. Interaction with stop conditions

The delivery model's stop conditions (§5) override everything here. After any stop, resumption requires the founder's explicit go recorded in the decision log — autonomy does not self-restart.
