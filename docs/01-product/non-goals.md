# Non-Goals

**Status:** Active. These are deliberate exclusions with reasons — the product is defined as much by what it refuses as by what it ships. Changing any entry requires a decision-log entry.

## Permanent non-goals *(change would contradict confirmed founder decisions)*

| # | Non-goal | Reason |
|---|----------|--------|
| N-01 | A tracker/logging/journaling product, or a dashboard-first home | D-002. The product guides; recording is downstream and purposeful. |
| N-02 | Required progress photographs, or photo prompts as engagement | D-003. Photos are optional per-programme tools only. |
| N-03 | Streak mechanics as motivation; guilt/shame/punishment anywhere | Founder requirement + evidence (Annex D §7: broken-streak salience demotivates; lapse framing decides outcomes). |
| N-04 | Fake urgency, fake discounts, countdown paywalls, manipulated scarcity | Founder brief's own trust positioning + UK DMCC/store policy (synthesis §5.1). |
| N-05 | Advertising use of user data; third-party ad SDKs | Privacy model; sensitive-adjacent data categories. |
| N-06 | Under-18 users or positioning | D-001/D-005: 18+ product. |
| N-07 | Permanent fitness-specific identity (language, navigation, engine, brand) | D-004: platform breadth preserved. |
| N-08 | Medical diagnosis, treatment plans, or outcome guarantees | Claims policy / governance framework; store health policies. |
| N-09 | Teeth-whitening programmes — *recommended permanent exclusion; formally confirmed with Q1* | Legally untenable as evidence-based content in UK/EU (synthesis §5.3); treated as excluded in all planning pending the Q1 decision-log record. |
| N-10 | AI-generated programme content without qualified human authorship and review | Governance: accountability requires named authors/reviewers; the anti-generic mandate. |

## Anti-scope tripwires

If any of these appears in a design or build review, stop and check this document: a new chart on Today; a new required field anywhere in the daily loop; a notification whose honest purpose is re-engagement rather than user value; a "quick win" that adds a category-specific string to universal UI; a feature justified mainly by "competitors have it".
