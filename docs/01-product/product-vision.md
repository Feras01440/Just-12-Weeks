# Product Vision

**Status:** Draft for founder review. Consistent with D-001–D-004. The vision is deliberately category-neutral (D-004).

## The one-sentence vision

> **A guided 12-week transformation platform: any adult with one meaningful goal gets told what to do today, shown how, supported through the hard weeks, and finishes with real evidence of change.**

## The world we're responding to *(Evidence-supported inference)*

Adults don't lack goals, information or apps. They lack **structure that survives contact with real life**. The gap between effort and visible results is where almost everyone quits: median time to habit automaticity is ~66 days (Lally 2010; Singh 2024), real-world 30-day retention for wellness apps sits near 3–4% (Baumel 2019), and the category's own tools make it worse — punishing lapses with broken streaks, demanding logging instead of giving direction, and paywalling before delivering value.

## What the product is

- **A guide, not a ledger** *(D-002)*: it tells, shows, guides and adapts before asking the user to record anything.
- **A commitment container**: twelve weeks, one goal, a real beginning, middle and end — and a designed way back in after every lapse.
- **Evidence-based and honest**: programmes grounded in published research with named review; progress defined per programme in terms that are actually true (capability, artefacts, milestones — not vanity metrics); no manufactured urgency anywhere.
- **For adults of every age** *(D-001)*: 18 to 80+, across digital confidence levels, without condescension in either direction.
- **Premium in craft**: calm, distinctive, instrument-like quality (the founder's Sleep Cycle benchmark) — because the product asks for trust and twelve weeks of someone's life.

## What success looks like in three years *(directional, not a commitment)*

A catalogue of deeply built programmes across physical, learning, practical/creative and personal-development archetypes; a user base whose defining story is "I actually finished"; completion reports people voluntarily share; a business sustained by honest renewals across successive 12-week journeys rather than by forgotten subscriptions.

## Fixed points (change only by decision-log entry)

1. Twelve weeks, one goal at a time.
2. Guidance before recording (D-002).
3. Recovery is designed, never punished.
4. Evidence-based content with accountable review.
5. Adult-inclusive by design (D-001).
6. No dark patterns — pricing, notifications, or retention.
7. Platform breadth preserved; no permanent category capture (D-004).

## Open points (deliberately undecided)

Flagship launch category (Q1) · monetisation model and pricing (Q5/Q6, Stage 1–2 evidence) · product name (Q10) · visual direction (Stage 3) · technology stack (ADR-001, provisional).
