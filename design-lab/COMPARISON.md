# How to Compare the Three Directions

This file is the reviewer’s route through the laboratory. Budget ~40 minutes for a fair pass.

## 1. The five-second test (5 min)

Open `#/compare/strength/today` (or press `0` then `w` to flip worlds). For each direction, without scrolling, answer aloud:
1. What should I do? 2. Why? 3. How do I begin? 4. What’s my alternative? 5. Where am I in the twelve weeks?

Then flip theme (`t`) and text scale (`s`) and repeat. A direction that fails at 200% text failed the test.

## 2. The signature walk (10 min)

Walk each direction through its live loop in one world:
`today → begin → active → paused → active → complete → question → acknowledge → journey`.
Watch what motion claims to mean. Then visit `week-transition` and `week-12`.

## 3. Recovery — the differentiator (10 min)

For each direction visit `missed-one`, `missed-several`, `long-absence`, `programme-pause` in **both worlds**. Ask: does this make me *more* or *less* likely to open the app after three silent weeks? Compare against the covenant in `docs/03-ux/recovery-pattern-comparison.md`.

## 4. The hard states (5 min)

`offline`, `loading`, `error`, `empty`, `expired`, `restore`, `subscription`. These are where template products give themselves away. Check: does the paywall respect you? Does expiry keep your work?

## 5. The masked-brand test (5 min)

Open `reports/comparison-index.html`. Cover the captions. For each row ask: could I tell which product this is with the logo removed? Could any cell pass for a generic habit app? (Findings from our internal pass are in `docs/04-brand/design-critique-report.md`.)

## 6. Type and evidence (5 min)

- `#/{a|b|c}/strength/specimen` at 100% and 200%, light and dark.
- `screenshots/` per direction; `reports/a11y-report.md` for the automated audit.

## Comparison questions worth arguing about afterwards

1. Which direction would you *miss* if it were deleted?
2. Which Today screen would you trust at 7am with low sleep?
3. Which recovery would bring you back after three weeks away — honestly?
4. Which twelve-expression still means something in week 9, when novelty is gone?
5. Which direction can a 68-year-old operate without being designed *at*?
6. Which direction is still itself in the writing world?

The decision-support scoring (deliberately not a verdict) lives in `docs/04-brand/experience-direction-comparison.md`.
