# Decisions Deliberately Not Made

This laboratory exists to make a later decision *better informed* — not to make it. The following are explicitly open, with what the lab contributes to each.

## Product & brand

1. **No winning direction.** Three directions are delivered at comparable fidelity with side-by-side evidence. The comparison document scores them against the sprint’s bars but declines a verdict.
2. **No flagship programme.** Both sample worlds (Beginner Strength Foundations, First-Draft Writing) run through every direction; elements safe regardless of flagship vs flagship-dependent elements are listed in the final report and comparison doc.
3. **No product name.** “12 Weeks / Twelve Weeks” is used as a working title exactly as the repository does; A’s half-title, B’s engraving and C’s warp all render a placeholder wordmark only.
4. **No final typography.** Three systems are explored with executable specimens; production requires on-device rendering checks (low-res Android), full language-coverage subsetting, and a Dynamic Type implementation pass. Literata is recorded as the safe body-serif fallback, not chosen.
5. **No final palette values.** Colour *logics* are established per direction; exact values would be re-derived under APCA as well as WCAG before production.
6. **No logo, iconography set, or illustration commissioning.** Imagery positions are defined (engraved plates / measured drawing / field-guide plates); actual asset production is post-direction work.

## Engineering

7. **No production mobile framework.** The lab is vanilla web on purpose; nothing about it endorses web views, React Native, Flutter, or native — that analysis belongs to the foundation track.
8. **No backend, accounts, sync, analytics, or notification system.** Local fixtures only. The offline states *assert* an offline-first posture; the architecture to honour it is undecided.
9. **No real subscription integration.** Commerce screens demonstrate ethical presentation only; store APIs, pricing tiers, regional pricing and trial mechanics are open. The £44.99/quarter figure is a placeholder for layout honesty.
10. **No haptic implementation.** Intent vocabulary documented; binding waits for the framework decision.

## Content & behaviour

11. **No final copy voice.** Fixture copy is sprint-quality to make the directions honest; a voice guide and clinical/safety review (especially strength-world language) are open.
12. **No coaching/adaptivity model.** The contextual question demonstrates *one purposeful question with a visible consequence*; the actual adaptation engine (what answers change what) is undesigned.
13. **No implementation-intention mechanics.** The research brief flags if-then session binding as the highest-leverage behaviour pattern; it appears only as copy hints, not as a designed flow.
14. **No notification/reminder strategy.** Calm-technology stance recorded; nothing designed.
15. **No pricing decision.** The ethical-presentation pattern is the deliverable, not the number.

## Process

16. **No user research conclusions.** All critiques are synthetic and labelled; 60–75 comprehension and post-lapse re-entry require real sessions before any direction is declared 65+-ready.
17. **No production accessibility conformance claim.** The lab demonstrates AA-targeted behaviour with automated evidence; a conformance audit belongs to the production build.
